# Case Studies

Evidence-graded studies of how the threat landscape is changing, written from primary sources: threat reports from AI providers and security vendors, government advisories, and the disclosures of the organizations that were hit. Each study is dated, because both the threats and the sources change.

Browse the timeline: [Case study timeline](/case-studies/TIMELINE.md) puts every study on one timeline with its dated key events, filterable by year, kind, sector, CVE and ATT&CK group.

## Method

Every study follows the same rules.

1. **Primary sources first.** Facts come from the report, advisory, or disclosure that first published them, linked to the exact page. Where only press coverage was available, the study says so.
2. **Every event carries an evidence grade.** The grade separates what has happened from what has only been shown to be possible.
3. **Hype and evidence are kept apart.** Each study says which threats are overstated and which are understated, and why.
4. **Numbers are reported as the source states them.** A study notes when a figure is a floor, an estimate, or a vendor's own count.
5. **Figures are checked against their sources.** Every figure in a study is checked against the source it cites.
6. **Every study is on the timeline.** Every new study adds its entry to [data/case_studies.yaml](https://github.com/TeamStarWolf/TeamStarWolf/blob/main/data/case_studies.yaml) (kind, period, sectors, CVEs, ATT&CK groups and up to 12 dated events from its own timeline) and reruns `scripts/build_case_study_timeline.py`; the check in CI fails otherwise.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | An observed, attributed incident with technical detail from the victim, a government agency, or the AI provider |
| Reported | A single vendor's account of in-the-wild activity, not independently corroborated |
| Demonstrated | A research proof of concept or a fixed vulnerability with no known in-the-wild use |

## About the figures

Figures are original charts and diagrams drawn from the data each study cites. Each figure's caption names its source. The how-to screenshots in the CVE-2026-98432 and Multipath TCP studies are renderings of real command output from a lab host running Kali 2026.2 with kernel 7.1.5, captured on 3 November 2026 with a neutral prompt. The screenshots in the npm supply-chain study are renderings of real command output from a throwaway `node:22.11.0-bookworm-slim` container on a lab host (npm 10.9.0), captured on 4 November 2026 UTC with the same neutral prompt; one has rows cut that carried package authors' contact details, and the study notes the cut. The screenshots in the Ghostcat study are renderings of real command output from Docker containers bound to loopback on a lab host (a vulnerable Tomcat 9.0.30 target and a 9.0.31 control), captured on 3 November 2026 with the same neutral prompt and the lab host anonymized; the setup figure is re-rendered from selected capture lines with the full text of the two probe files omitted, and the study's captions say so. The screenshots in the ProFTPD mod_copy study are renderings of real command output from two loopback-only ProFTPD containers (a build just before the CVE-2015-3306 fix and a build of the fix merge) on a lab host, captured on 4 November 2026 UTC with the same neutral prompt; the exploit-class test capture is not shown, and in the mitigation screenshot the copy command is replaced by a one-line description.

## About the downloads

Studies are exported to PDF, and to a slide deck in PowerPoint format once a slide plan is written for them. Both are generated from the study's Markdown by `scripts/export_case_study.py`: `pdf` writes one study's PDF to `case-studies/pdf`, `all-pdf` writes the PDF of every study in the table above that lacks one, and `pptx` renders a slide plan from `case-studies/slides/plans` to `case-studies/slides`. A slide plan is a short Markdown page: the deck title, the study it draws on, the cover title, a subtitle and the published date, then one section per slide whose heading carries a kicker and a narrative title, with bullets, an optional two-column split, an optional row of up to four numbers with captions, an optional table, and a notes line for the source numbers. The exporter measures every text box against a character budget and refuses a plan that would overflow; the script's docstring has the full format, and the [Ghostcat slide plan](/case-studies/slides/plans/GHOSTCAT_CVE_2020_1938.md) is the model; the [SolarWinds and SUNBURST](/case-studies/slides/plans/SOLARWINDS_SUNBURST.md), [2019 chronicle](/case-studies/slides/plans/CHRONICLE_2019.md), [Log4Shell](/case-studies/slides/plans/LOG4SHELL.md), [ICS attacks and advisories](/case-studies/slides/plans/ICS_ATTACKS_AND_ADVISORIES_2024_2026.md), [npm supply-chain attacks](/case-studies/slides/plans/NPM_SUPPLY_CHAIN_ATTACKS.md), [Hugging Face supply chain](/case-studies/slides/plans/HUGGING_FACE_SUPPLY_CHAIN.md), [APT41 public record](/case-studies/slides/plans/APT41_PUBLIC_RECORD.md), [Change Healthcare 2024](/case-studies/slides/plans/CHANGE_HEALTHCARE_2024.md), [Stuxnet](/case-studies/slides/plans/STUXNET.md), [ProFTPD mod_copy](/case-studies/slides/plans/PROFTPD_MOD_COPY_CVE_2015_3306.md), [2020 chronicle](/case-studies/slides/plans/CHRONICLE_2020.md), [Exchange Marauder 2021](/case-studies/slides/plans/EXCHANGE_MARAUDER_2021.md), [Sandworm operations](/case-studies/slides/plans/SANDWORM_OPERATIONS.md), [China-attributed threat actors](/case-studies/slides/plans/THREAT_ACTORS_CHINA.md), [Russia-attributed threat actors](/case-studies/slides/plans/THREAT_ACTORS_RUSSIA.md), [other state-linked threat actors](/case-studies/slides/plans/THREAT_ACTORS_OTHER_STATES.md), [United States-attributed threat actors](/case-studies/slides/plans/THREAT_ACTORS_UNITED_STATES.md), [Iran-attributed threat actors](/case-studies/slides/plans/THREAT_ACTORS_IRAN.md), [2021 chronicle](/case-studies/slides/plans/CHRONICLE_2021.md) and [North Korea-attributed threat actors](/case-studies/slides/plans/THREAT_ACTORS_NORTH_KOREA.md) plans follow it.
