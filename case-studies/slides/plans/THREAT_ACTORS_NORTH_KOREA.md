## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY | One state program funds itself by theft, and the record names it

- **The attribution is well documented:** US charges in 2018, 2020 and 2024, and Treasury designations in 2018, 2019, 2023 and 2025.
- **The money is the mission:** MSMT counted at least $1.19 billion in crypto stolen in 2024.
- **Healthcare is a deliberate target:** Maui ransomware has hit US healthcare since at least May 2021.
- **Thousands of IT workers** hired under false identities now extort employers with stolen code.
- **The names do not line up:** "Lazarus Group" is an umbrella, and vendors split the same activity.
Caption: Grades: court records, sanctions, advisories, ATT&CK pages and vendor reports Confirmed; the Binance dispute Reported.

## PART 2 | Four eras: punishment, bank robbery, crypto, then insiders

- **2009 to 2016:** espionage and punishment, with DDoS, the 2013 bank and media attacks and Sony Pictures.
- **2016 to 2019:** Bangladesh Bank, FASTCash and the first exchange thefts turn the program to revenue.
- **2020 to 2022:** trojanized trading apps, bridge thefts, vaccine targeting and Maui arrive together.
- **2023 to 2026:** 3CX, fake job interviews, the IT worker scheme and thefts above $1 billion.

## DEFENDER VIEW | Verify people, patch on the KEV clock, and hunt the advisories

Left: Controls
- Verify identity through employment, not only at hiring.
- Patch internet-facing hospital systems on the KEV clock.
- Set DMARC to p=quarantine or p=reject.
- Keep offline, immutable and tested backups.
Right: Red flags and hunts
- Refusal to appear on video; laptops sent to forwarders.
- One account logging in from several countries.
- mshta launching HTA files; PowerShell run without powershell.exe.
- Zeroed firewall registry values; HWP association changes.
Caption: The FBI's October 2026 PSA calls the approach "identity in depth".
Notes: IT workers [48][54][66][72][79]. KEV patching [41][60][83]. DMARC [53]; Kimsuky impersonation [44]. Backups and healthcare playbook [38][41]. Kimsuky detections [26]. Crypto guidance [35][68]. Treating red flags as security incidents is Inferred.

## CROSS-CUTTING THEMES | Trust is the attack surface, and names split faster than activity

- **Revenue drives the program:** US agencies and MSMT tie the proceeds to weapons programs.
- **Trust is the attack surface:** recruiters, job tests, trading apps, messenger updates and hiring itself.
- **One unit, two missions:** scope a hospital ransomware case as possible espionage too.
- **Attribution is a ladder:** from "able to confirm" down to "likely theory" and "aligned".
- **Overstated:** "Lazarus did it". **Understated:** healthcare as a target and the IT worker program.
