# AML.M0002: Predictive AI Output Obfuscation

<a id="amlm0002"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0002](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0002), addresses 10 techniques

Reduce the fidelity and amount of information returned by predictive AI inference endpoints to make model discovery, extraction, replication, and black-box adversarial-example optimization more difficult. Limit outputs to those required by the application. Depending on the use case, this may include withholding or reducing the precision of confidence scores, logits, class rankings, labels, embeddings, or additional model metadata.
