# AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data

<a id="amlm0007"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0007](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0007), addresses 7 techniques

Detect, quarantine, and remove or remediate poisoned data before it is used to train an AI model. Apply screening when datasets are acquired or updated, and before new data is incorporated into active learning workflows. Inspect data samples, annotations, and metadata for evidence of manipulation. Depending on the data and intended task, checks may include label inconsistencies, anomalous samples, unexpected shifts in source or class distributions, suspicious duplication or repeated patterns, and backdoor triggers. Compare findings with trusted dataset versions and combine automated screening with targeted review. Quarantine suspicious records while they are investigated. Remove malicious additions, correct affected labels or annotations, or restore data from a trusted version where appropriate. Repeat screening after remediation and before utilizing the dataset for training tasks. If affected data has already been used for training, identify the resulting models and evaluate whether retraining, rollback, or other corrective action is needed. Combine sanitization with Maintain AI Dataset Provenance and Validate AI Model. Tools that support screening and sanitizing training data include: - Adversarial Robustness Toolbox (ART) - Cleanlab
