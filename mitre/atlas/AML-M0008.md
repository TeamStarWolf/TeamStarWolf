# AML.M0008: Validate AI Model

<a id="amlm0008"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0008](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0008), addresses 18 techniques

Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle. Validate newly trained or acquired models and repeat evaluation after fine-tuning, model updates, or changes to the conditions in which the model is used. Use representative evaluation data and adversarial test cases appropriate to the model's capabilities and intended use. Test for unexpected behavior associated with poisoned training data, model tampering, backdoor triggers, adversarial inputs, and sensitive information disclosure. For generative models, include evaluations of susceptibility to prompt injection and jailbreaks, adherence to authorized instructions, and disclosure of information supplied in context or retained through training. Compare results against trusted baselines or previously evaluated model versions, and investigate significant regressions in performance or unexplained behavior. Use findings to inform model selection, remediation, deployment restrictions, or rollback, and repeat affected evaluations after corrective changes. During deployment, monitor for changes in model performance and use significant changes to trigger revalidation. Data or concept drift may result from legitimate changes in the operating environment and should be investigated alongside evidence of poisoning or tampering.
