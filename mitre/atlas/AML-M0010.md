# AML.M0010: Predictive AI Input Restoration

<a id="amlm0010"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0010](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0010), addresses 8 techniques

Preprocess predictive AI inference inputs to remove, reduce, or disrupt adversarial perturbations before the inputs are evaluated by the model. Restoration methods may include denoising, compression, reconstruction, resampling, feature squeezing, randomized transformations, or other modality-appropriate preprocessing. Evaluate restoration methods against adaptive adversaries that account for the preprocessing operation.
