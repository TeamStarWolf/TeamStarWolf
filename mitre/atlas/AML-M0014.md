# AML.M0014: Verify AI Artifacts

<a id="amlm0014"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0014](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0014), addresses 13 techniques

Verify the integrity and authenticity of AI artifacts before importing, loading, or deploying them. Artifacts may include model weights and checkpoints, datasets, tokenizers, model configurations, prompt templates, and agent configuration files. Verify the complete set of files used by the AI system, including supporting files that can alter model or agent behavior. Compare cryptographic hashes against expected values obtained from an authenticated publisher. Where digital signatures or signed manifests are available, verify that they cover the artifacts being used and were produced by an authorized signer. Do not rely solely on checksums distributed alongside an artifact through the same untrusted or compromised source. Pin approved artifacts to specific versions and cryptographic digests rather than relying on mutable names, tags, or download locations. Verify artifacts when they are acquired, transferred, or updated, and before they are loaded or interpreted. Successful verification establishes that an artifact matches a trusted reference or was signed by an accepted identity. It does not establish that the artifact is free of malicious code, poisoned data, backdoors, or harmful instructions. Combine artifact verification with Vulnerability Scanning and Validate AI Model.
