# AML.M0023: Maintain an AI Bill of Materials

<a id="amlm0023"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0023](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0023), addresses 9 techniques

Create and maintain an AI Bill of Materials (AI BOM) that identifies the components and dependencies used to build, evaluate, and operate an AI-enabled system. Include models and checkpoints, datasets, software packages, inference runtimes, container images, and agent configurations including system instructions and connected tools and data sources. For systems that use external AI services or agent tools, record the relevant providers, services, integrations, and available version identifiers. Record component identifiers, versions, sources, suppliers or publishers, and cryptographic digests. Capture dependency and derivation relationships, such as which base model and dataset versions produced a fine-tuned model. Link to supporting provenance, integrity-verification, and evaluation records rather than treating inclusion in the AI BOM as evidence of approval or safety. Generate or update the AI BOM when components are acquired, built, modified, or deployed. Retain version history and protect records from unauthorized modification. Identify gaps where upstream information or hosted-service version details are unavailable. Use the AI BOM to assess component changes, correlate security advisories and reports of compromised artifacts with deployed systems, and trace affected dependencies and derived models. Use these findings to prioritize investigation, replacement, rollback, or retraining. An AI BOM provides inventory and traceability. It does not establish that a listed component is authentic, unmodified, or free of malicious behavior. Combine it with Verify AI Artifacts, Maintain AI Dataset Provenance, and Validate AI Model.
