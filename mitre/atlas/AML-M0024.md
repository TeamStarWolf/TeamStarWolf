# AML.M0024: AI Telemetry Logging and Monitoring

<a id="amlm0024"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0024](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0024), addresses 20 techniques

Implement logging and monitoring across the AI lifecycle to detect suspicious activity and support investigation. Capture security-relevant access, changes, and operations across data pipelines, model development, deployment, and connected systems. Preserve sufficient context to trace activity across components and identify coordinated behavior across accounts and access pathways. Tailor telemetry to each system's function, including relevant data and model versions, request and execution metadata, and operation results. For agentic systems, distinguish requested actions from those actually executed, using independent service records where available. Treat model-generated explanations and summaries as supporting context rather than authoritative evidence of execution. Monitor individual events and patterns over time for anomalous access, unauthorized changes, unusual usage, and unexpected model behavior or external actions. Correlate findings with related operational activity to guide investigation and response. Protect telemetry from unauthorized access, modification, or deletion, and monitor for gaps in coverage. Limit the collection of sensitive content through metadata, references, or redaction where these preserve investigative value, and apply appropriate retention controls.
