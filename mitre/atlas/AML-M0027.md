# AML.M0027 — Single-User AI Agent Permissions Configuration

<a id="amlm0027"></a>

**ATLAS:** [AML.M0027](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0027) · addresses 7 techniques

When deploying an AI agent that acts as a representative of a user and performs actions on their behalf, it is important to implement robust policies and controls on permissions and lifecycle management of the agent. Lifecycle management involves establishing identity, protocols for access management, and decommissioning of the agent when its role is no longer needed.
