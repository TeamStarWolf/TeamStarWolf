# AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation

<a id="amlm0040"></a>

ATLAS: [AML.M0040](https://atlas.mitre.org/mitigations/AML.M0040), addresses 12 techniques

Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected. Based on the assessed confidence and severity of the activity, apply targeted response modifications that limit the information available to an adversary while preserving legitimate functionality where possible. Response degradation techniques may include: - Routing suspicious requests to less capable or more constrained models - Reducing output precision, specificity, or completeness - Withholding unnecessary reasoning details, confidence information, or auxiliary outputs - Restricting access to high-fidelity or structured responses - Introducing carefully controlled output perturbations These measures can reduce the value of collected responses as training data, limit an adversary's ability to infer model behavior or capabilities, reduce disclosure of information about the model's training data, and increase the cost and difficulty of replicating a target model. Apply response degradation proportionally to the assessed risk of adversarial query activity, using behavioral indicators and detection confidence to minimize disruption to legitimate users. Evaluate degradation mechanisms to ensure they do not introduce misleading or unsafe outputs, inadvertently disclose detection decisions, or undermine the reliability of authorized interactions.
