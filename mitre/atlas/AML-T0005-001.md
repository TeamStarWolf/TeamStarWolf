# AML.T0005.001: Replicate Behavior from Model Outputs

<a id="amlt0005001"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development, ai-attack-adaptation  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0005: Replicate AI Model Behavior](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0005.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0005.001](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0005.001)  

Adversaries may replicate an AI model's behavior or selected capabilities by training or adapting another model using outputs or other sources of supervision obtained from the target. Supervisory material may include labels, scores, probabilities, generated responses, reasoning explanations, evaluations, reward or preference signals, and tool-use or agent interaction trajectories. The adversary may use supervised learning, knowledge distillation, supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, or related methods. Target-generated material may be filtered, combined with other data, or acquired from multiple teacher models. The resulting model may reproduce selected capabilities rather than the target's full behavior, architecture, parameters, or output ontology.

## Mitigations (4)

- [AML.M0002: Predictive AI Output Obfuscation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0002.md): Reduce the fidelity and amount of information returned by predictive AI inference endpoints to make model discovery, extraction, replication, and black-box adversarial-example optimization more difficult.
- [AML.M0004: Limit AI Service Query Volume and Rate](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0004.md): Limit the number and rate of requests that users can submit to an AI service.
- [AML.M0024: AI Telemetry Logging and Monitoring](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0024.md): Implement logging and monitoring across the AI lifecycle to detect suspicious activity and support investigation.
- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
