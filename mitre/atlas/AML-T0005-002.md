# AML.T0005.002: Adapt Existing Model to Replicate Behavior

<a id="amlt0005002"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development, ai-attack-adaptation  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0005: Replicate AI Model Behavior](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0005.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0005.002](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0005.002)  

Adversaries may select an existing AI model whose behavior resembles a target and adapt it to more closely reproduce the capabilities or responses of interest. They may fine-tune the model or adjust its prompts and inference settings to improve that similarity. The resulting model may serve as a surrogate for attack development or provide a reusable capability that replicates selected target behavior.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0000: Limit Public Release of Information](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0000.md): Limit the public release of technical information about the AI stack used in an organization's products or services.
