# AML.T0005: Replicate AI Model Behavior

<a id="amlt0005"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development, ai-attack-adaptation  
ATLAS: [AML.T0005](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0005)  

Adversaries may replicate an AI model's behavior or selected capabilities by training or adapting another model to approximate the target's responses, decision regions, or other characteristics. The result may be a surrogate for attack development, a functional replica, or a student model incorporating selected target capabilities. Replication may cover a whole model or only the behaviors relevant to the adversary's objective. Models may be developed from gathered artifacts, collected model outputs, or adaptation of an existing model. How closely the model must reproduce the target's behavior depends on the adversary's goals. Replicate AI Model Behavior supports adversaries' ability to achieve Resource Development when producing a reusable model capability and AI Attack Adaptation when tailoring a model for a particular target or operation.

## Mitigations (6)

- [AML.M0000: Limit Public Release of Information](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0000.md): Limit the public release of technical information about the AI stack used in an organization's products or services.
- [AML.M0001: Limit Model Artifact Release](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0001.md): Limit public release of technical project details including data, algorithms, model architectures, and model checkpoints that are used in production, or that are representative of those used in production.
- [AML.M0002: Predictive AI Output Obfuscation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0002.md): Reduce the fidelity and amount of information returned by predictive AI inference endpoints to make model discovery, extraction, replication, and black-box adversarial-example optimization more difficult.
- [AML.M0004: Limit AI Service Query Volume and Rate](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0004.md): Limit the number and rate of requests that users can submit to an AI service.
- [AML.M0019: Control Access to AI Models and Data in Production](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0019.md): Require users to verify their identities before accessing a production model.
- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
