# AML.T0006.003: Probe AI Agent Trigger Channels

<a id="amlt0006003"></a>

ATLAS tactics: reconnaissance  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0006: Active Scanning](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0006.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0006.003](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0006.003)  

Adversaries may send crafted inputs to potential public triggers, such as email addresses, webhooks, or messaging channels, to elicit a response that indicates an invocation of agentic activity. The nature of the response and its content can often reveal agent-managed accounts and expose the additional agentic attack surface. Identified triggers can be used to directly attack the agent.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0039: AI Honeypots](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0039.md): Deploy monitored decoy AI services, agents, credentials, or other resources that resemble legitimate, active assets in the external or internal environment, but serve no real business or operational functions.
