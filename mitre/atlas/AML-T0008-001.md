# AML.T0008.001: Consumer Hardware

<a id="amlt0008001"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0008: Acquire Infrastructure](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0008.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0008.001](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0008.001)  

Adversaries may acquire consumer hardware to conduct their attacks. Owning the hardware provides the adversary with complete control of the environment. These devices can be hard to trace.
