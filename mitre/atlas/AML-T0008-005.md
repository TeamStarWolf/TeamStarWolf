# AML.T0008.005: AI Service Proxies

<a id="amlt0008005"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0008: Acquire Infrastructure](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0008.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0008.005](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0008.005)  

Adversaries may acquire proxy infrastructure that mediates access to AI services. Proxies can provide a common interface to multiple upstream accounts or providers while concealing underlying credentials from downstream users. Adversaries may purchase access to existing proxy services from commercial operators or gray-market resellers, or deploy publicly available proxy software on their own infrastructure. Proxy infrastructure may route requests across provider APIs, cloud endpoints, aggregators, and account pools based on service health, availability, quotas, or cost, with failover when access is disrupted. Operators may rotate or replace accounts and mix coordinated requests with unrelated customer traffic, enabling access from restricted regions as well as complicating detection and attribution (See Centralized AI Service Request Routing)..Adversaries may deliberately utilize proxies for Defense Evasion (See Centralized AI Service Request Routing and AI Service Request Metadata Sanitization). AI service proxy infrastructure may be used as "transfer stations" for gray-market resale. Their upstream suppliers may Establish Accounts through bulk registration, supported by verification and payment intermediaries, while operators pool capacity from promotional credits, discounted access, unused quotas, or shared subscriptions. Proxy infrastructure may also use Valid Accounts obtained through credential theft as observed in LLMjacking campaigns. Adversaries have used proxy services to route requests and collect responses for model distillation campaigns. Adversaries may collect Outputs from AI Models and use them to Replicate Behavior from Model Outputs.
