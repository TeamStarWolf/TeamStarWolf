# AML.T0010.001 — AI Software

<a id="amlt0010001"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** initial-access  
**Sub-technique of:** [AML.T0010 — AI Supply Chain Compromise](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0010.md)  
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0010.001](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0010.001)  

Adversaries may target software packages that are commonly used in AI-enabled systems or are part of the AI DevOps lifecycle. This can include deep learning frameworks used to build AI models (e.g. PyTorch, TensorFlow, Jax), generative AI integration frameworks (e.g. LangChain, LangFlow), inference engines, and AI DevOps tools. They may also target the dependency chains of any of these software packages [\[1\]][1].
