# AML.T0010.003: Model Repository

<a id="amlt0010003"></a>

ATLAS tactics: initial-access  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0010: Supply Chain Compromise](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0010.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0010.003](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0010.003)  

Adversaries may abuse model repositories or other model distribution services to introduce compromised models into downstream AI systems. They may replace published artifacts, manipulate model versions or references that consumers resolve, or publish malicious models under identities or locations that consumers trust. The repository or distribution service need not itself be compromised. Adversaries may use legitimate publishing functionality or control an upstream account, namespace, or artifact referenced by a downstream workflow. Consumers may retrieve the compromised model for inference, fine-tuning, or incorporation into another application.

## Mitigations (8)

- [AML.M0005: Control Access to AI Models and Data at Rest](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0005.md): Establish access controls on internal model registries and limit internal access to production models.
- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0013: Code Signing](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0013.md): Enforce binary and application integrity with digital signature verification to prevent untrusted code from executing.
- [AML.M0014: Verify AI Artifacts](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0014.md): Verify the integrity and authenticity of AI artifacts before importing, loading, or deploying them.
- [AML.M0017: AI Model Distribution Methods](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0017.md): Deploying AI models to edge devices can increase the attack surface of the system.
- [AML.M0023: Maintain an AI Bill of Materials](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0023.md): Create and maintain an AI Bill of Materials (AI BOM) that identifies the components and dependencies used to build, evaluate, and operate an AI-enabled system.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
- [AML.M0042: Threat Intelligence Program](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0042.md): A Threat Intelligence Program enables organizations to proactively collect, analyze, and share information on threats to AI and cyber systems by leveraging internal observations and external reporting.
