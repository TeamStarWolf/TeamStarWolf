# AML.T0015 — Evade AI Model

<a id="amlt0015"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** initial-access, defense-evasion, impact
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0015](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0015)

Adversaries can Craft Adversarial Data that prevents an AI model from correctly identifying the contents of the data or Generate Deepfakes that fools an AI model expecting authentic data. This technique can be used to evade a downstream task where AI is utilized. The adversary may evade AI-based virus/malware detection or network scanning towards the goal of a traditional cyber attack. AI model evasion through deepfake generation may also provide initial access to systems that use AI-based biometric authentication.

## Mitigations (6)

- [AML.M0003 — Predictive AI Model Hardening](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0003.md) — Use techniques to make AI models robust to adversarial inputs such as adversarial training or network distillation.
- [AML.M0006 — Predictive AI Ensembles](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0006.md) — Use an ensemble of models for inference to increase robustness to adversarial inputs.
- [AML.M0009 — Predictive AI Multi-Sensor Fusion](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0009.md) — Incorporate multiple sensors to integrate varying perspectives and modalities to avoid a single point of failure susceptible to physical attacks.
- [AML.M0010 — Predictive AI Input Restoration](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0010.md) — Preprocess all inference data to nullify or reverse potential adversarial perturbations.
- [AML.M0015 — Predictive AI Adversarial Input Detection](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0015.md) — Detect and block adversarial inputs or atypical queries that deviate from known benign behavior, exhibit behavior patterns observed in previous attacks or that come from potentially malicious IPs.
- [AML.M0034 — Deepfake Detection](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0034.md) — Apply deepfake detection algorithms against any untrusted or user-provided data, especially in impactful applications such as biometric verification, to block generated content.
