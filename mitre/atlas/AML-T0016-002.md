# AML.T0016.002 — Generative AI

<a id="amlt0016002"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** resource-development
**Sub-technique of:** [AML.T0016](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0016.md)
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0016.002](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0016.002)

Adversaries may search for and obtain generative AI models or tools, such as large language models (LLMs), to assist them in various steps of their operation. Generative AI can be used in a variety of malicious ways, such as to generating malware, to Generate Deepfakes, to Generate Malicious Commands, for Retrieval Content Crafting, or to generate Phishing content. Adversaries may obtain open source models and serve them locally using frameworks such as Ollama or vLLM. They may host them using cloud infrastructure.
