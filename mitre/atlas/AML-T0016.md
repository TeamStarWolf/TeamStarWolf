# AML.T0016: Obtain Capabilities

<a id="amlt0016"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
ATLAS: [AML.T0016](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0016)  

Adversaries may search for and obtain software capabilities for use in their operations. Capabilities may be specific to AI-based attacks Adversarial AI Attack Implementations or generic software tools repurposed for malicious intent (Software Tools). In both instances, an adversary may modify or customize the capability to aid in targeting a particular AI-enabled system.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0001: Limit Model Artifact Release](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0001.md): Limit public release of technical project details including data, algorithms, model architectures, and model checkpoints that are used in production, or that are representative of those used in production.
