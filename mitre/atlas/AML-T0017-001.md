# AML.T0017.001: Autonomous Exploit Development

<a id="amlt0017001"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0017: Develop Capabilities](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0017.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0017.001](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0017.001)  

An autonomous AI agent may identify a software vulnerability and develop or materially adapt an exploit capability with limited human direction. The agent may analyze source code, documentation, service behavior, and error responses to infer a vulnerability and the conditions required to exploit it. The agent may formulate and test vulnerability hypotheses, generate probes or payloads, interpret the results, and revise its approach through repeated action-observation cycles. It may combine multiple weaknesses into an exploit chain or package the resulting capability for later use. Validation may establish that the exploit produces the intended access, code execution, or other technical effect. The vulnerability may be publicly known or previously unknown.

## Mitigations (2)

- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
- [AML.M0042: Threat Intelligence Program](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0042.md): A Threat Intelligence Program enables organizations to proactively collect, analyze, and share information on threats to AI and cyber systems by leveraging internal observations and external reporting.
