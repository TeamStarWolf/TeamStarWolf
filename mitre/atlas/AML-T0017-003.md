# AML.T0017.003: Malware

<a id="amlt0017003"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0017: Develop Capabilities](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0017.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0017.003](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0017.003)  

Adversaries may develop malware to support their operations. This may include creating or modifying malicious scripts, executables, libraries, or software packages to enable unauthorized access, execution, credential theft, collection, exfiltration, or disruption. Malware may be developed as a standalone capability or embedded within otherwise legitimate-looking software, such as a package that performs malicious actions when installed or imported. Adversaries may use AI models or autonomous AI agents to generate malicious code, adapt existing implementations, and iteratively test and refine functionality. AI agents may translate high-level operational objectives into malware requirements. Development may include preparing the malicious code as an installable package for subsequent staging and delivery.
