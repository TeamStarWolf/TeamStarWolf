# AML.T0018.000: Poison AI Model

<a id="amlt0018000"></a>

ATLAS tactics: ai-attack-adaptation, persistence  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0018: Manipulate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0018.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0018.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0018.000)  

Adversaries may manipulate an AI model's weights to change it's behavior or performance, resulting in a poisoned model. Adversaries may poison a model by directly manipulating its weights, training the model on poisoned data, further fine-tuning the model, or otherwise interfering with its training process. The change in behavior of poisoned models may be limited to targeted categories in predictive AI models, or targeted topics, concepts, or facts in generative AI models, or aim for a general performance degradation.

## Mitigations (8)

- [AML.M0005: Control Access to AI Models and Data at Rest](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0005.md): Establish access controls on internal model registries and limit internal access to production models.
- [AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0007.md): Detect, quarantine, and remove or remediate poisoned data before it is used to train an AI model.
- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0013: Code Signing](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0013.md): Enforce binary and application integrity with digital signature verification to prevent untrusted code from executing.
- [AML.M0014: Verify AI Artifacts](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0014.md): Verify the integrity and authenticity of AI artifacts before importing, loading, or deploying them.
- [AML.M0023: Maintain an AI Bill of Materials](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0023.md): Create and maintain an AI Bill of Materials (AI BOM) that identifies the components and dependencies used to build, evaluate, and operate an AI-enabled system.
- [AML.M0025: Maintain AI Dataset Provenance](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0025.md): Maintain a detailed history of datasets used for AI applications.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
