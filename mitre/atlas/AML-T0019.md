# AML.T0019 — Publish Poisoned Datasets

<a id="amlt0019"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** resource-development
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0019](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0019)

Adversaries may publish data affected by Training Data Poisoning to a public location. The poisoned dataset may be a novel dataset or a poisoned variant of an existing open source dataset. This data may be introduced to a victim system via AI Supply Chain Compromise.
