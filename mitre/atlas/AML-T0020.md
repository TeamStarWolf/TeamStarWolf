# AML.T0020 — Training Data Poisoning

<a id="amlt0020"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** resource-development, persistence
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0020](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0020)

Adversaries alter training or fine-tuning samples, labels, or feedback to bias model behavior, impair performance, or introduce backdoors.
