# AML.T0020: Training Data Poisoning

<a id="amlt0020"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development, persistence  
ATLAS: [AML.T0020](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0020)  

Adversaries alter training or fine-tuning samples, labels, or feedback to bias model behavior, impair performance, or introduce backdoors.

## Mitigations (6)

- [AML.M0001: Limit Model Artifact Release](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0001.md): Limit public release of technical project details including data, algorithms, model architectures, and model checkpoints that are used in production, or that are representative of those used in production.
- [AML.M0005: Control Access to AI Models and Data at Rest](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0005.md): Establish access controls on internal model registries and limit internal access to production models.
- [AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0007.md): Detect and remove or remediate poisoned training data.
- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Validate that AI models perform as intended by testing for backdoor triggers, potential for data leakage, or adversarial influence.
- [AML.M0023: AI Bill of Materials](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0023.md): An AI Bill of Materials (AI BOM) contains a full listing of artifacts and resources that were used in building the AI.
- [AML.M0025: Maintain AI Dataset Provenance](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0025.md): Maintain a detailed history of datasets used for AI applications.
