# AML.T0021 — Establish Accounts

<a id="amlt0021"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** resource-development  
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0021](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0021)  

Adversaries may create accounts with various services for use in targeting, to gain access to resources needed in AI Attack Adaptation, or for victim impersonation.
