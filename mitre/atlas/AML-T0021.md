# AML.T0021: Establish Accounts

<a id="amlt0021"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
ATLAS: [AML.T0021](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0021)  

Adversaries may create accounts with various services for use in targeting, to gain access to resources needed to further their operations, or for victim impersonation. Adversaries may establish pools of AI service accounts through bulk or fraudulent registration. These accounts may exploit promotional credits, educational or startup programs, or premium subscriptions. They may register accounts directly or use account merchants and third parties that supply registration resources, such as SMS verification services and payment arrangements. Registration arrangements may obscure the operator's country of origin, and related accounts may share registration attributes or payment instruments. Established accounts can supply upstream access for AI Service Proxies to serve as "transfer stations" for gray-market resellers. Adversaries may also purposefully route requests through proxies for Centralized AI Service Request Routing to avoid policy enforcement and evade detection. Adversaries or autonomous AI agents may establish accounts and cultivate personas on social media (e.g. Facebook, LinkedIn, X), software hosting (e.g. GitHub, GitLab), or AI artifact hosting (e.g. Hugging Face) sites. Account creation supplies the identities used for subsequent Impersonation and Phishing activities. Autonomous AI agents may independently select services and create accounts as prerequisites for subsequent attack activity, without a human directing each registration. Agents may obtain verification messages through controlled mailboxes, switch proxies to overcome account registration restrictions, or use visual recognition and audio matching to solve human verification challenges such as CAPTCHAs.

## Mitigations (3)

- [AML.M0005: Control Access to AI Models and Data at Rest](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0005.md): Establish access controls on internal model registries and limit internal access to production models.
- [AML.M0019: Control Access to AI Models and Data in Production](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0019.md): Require users to verify their identities before accessing a production model.
- [AML.M0043: AI Agent External Resource Lifecycle Management](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0043.md): Organizations should control external resources created or acquired by AI agents during execution by treating those resources as attributable, lifecycle-managed assets.
