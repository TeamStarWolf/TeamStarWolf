# AML.T0024: Exfiltration via AI Inference API

<a id="amlt0024"></a>

ATLAS tactics: exfiltration  
ATLAS: [AML.T0024](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0024)  

Adversaries may exfiltrate information from a victim-controlled AI model or service through responses returned by its inference API. Transferred information may include predictions, scores, embeddings, generated content (e.g. text or images), or other sensitive information disclosed in model responses. Responses may be received directly or relayed through an intermediary. Adversaries may submit inputs designed to cause information to be returned through ordinary API response fields or streaming outputs. The inference API response serves as the transfer channel, including when adversaries use valid accounts or credentials to obtain responses as part of an unauthorized extraction operation. Received responses may be gathered as Outputs from AI Models, analyzed to obtain Training Data Information from AI Models, used to Craft Adversarial Data, or to Replicate Behavior from Model Outputs.

## Mitigations (4)

- [AML.M0004: Limit AI Service Query Volume and Rate](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0004.md): Limit the number and rate of requests that users can submit to an AI service.
- [AML.M0019: Control Access to AI Models and Data in Production](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0019.md): Require users to verify their identities before accessing a production model.
- [AML.M0024: AI Telemetry Logging and Monitoring](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0024.md): Implement logging and monitoring across the AI lifecycle to detect suspicious activity and support investigation.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
