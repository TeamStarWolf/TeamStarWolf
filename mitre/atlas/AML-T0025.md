# AML.T0025: Exfiltration via Cyber Means

<a id="amlt0025"></a>

ATLAS tactics: exfiltration  
ATLAS: [AML.T0025](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0025)  

Adversaries may exfiltrate AI artifacts or other information relevant to their goals via traditional cyber means. See the ATT&CK Exfiltration tactic for more information.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0005: Control Access to AI Models and Data at Rest](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0005.md): Establish access controls on internal model registries and limit internal access to production models.
