# AML.T0035 — AI Artifact Collection

<a id="amlt0035"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** collection
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0035](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0035)

Adversaries may collect AI artifacts for Exfiltration or for use in AI Attack Adaptation. AI artifacts include models and datasets as well as other telemetry data produced when interacting with a model.
