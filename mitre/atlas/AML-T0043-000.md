# AML.T0043.000 — White-Box Optimization

<a id="amlt0043000"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** ai-attack-adaptation
**Sub-technique of:** [AML.T0043 — Craft Adversarial Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0043.md)
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0043.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0043.000)

In White-Box Optimization, the adversary has full access to the target model and optimizes the adversarial example directly. Adversarial examples trained in this manner are most effective against the target model.
