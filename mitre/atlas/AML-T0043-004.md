# AML.T0043.004: Insert Backdoor Trigger

<a id="amlt0043004"></a>

ATLAS tactics: ai-attack-adaptation  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0043: Craft Adversarial Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0043.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0043.004](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0043.004)  

The adversary may add a perceptual trigger into inference data. The trigger may be imperceptible or non-obvious to humans. This technique is used in conjunction with Poison AI Model and allows the adversary to produce their desired effect in the target model.

## Mitigations (5)

- [AML.M0003: Predictive AI Model Hardening](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0003.md): Design and train predictive AI models to maintain intended performance when presented with adversarial examples.
- [AML.M0006: Predictive AI Ensembles](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0006.md): Use an ensemble of diverse predictive AI models to reduce reliance on a single model or model family and improve robustness against adversarial examples.
- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0010: Predictive AI Input Restoration](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0010.md): Preprocess predictive AI inference inputs to remove, reduce, or disrupt adversarial perturbations before the inputs are evaluated by the model.
- [AML.M0015: Predictive AI Adversarial Input Detection](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0015.md): Detect and block digital or physical adversarial examples submitted to predictive AI models.
