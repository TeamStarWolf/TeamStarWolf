# AML.T0048.004: AI Intellectual Property Theft

<a id="amlt0048004"></a>

ATLAS tactics: impact  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0048: External Harms](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0048.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0048.004](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0048.004)  

Adversaries may appropriate AI-related intellectual property in a manner that causes economic or competitive harm to the victim organization. Targeted material may include model architectures and weights, training data, or model behaviors and capabilities. Adversaries may steal AI models or other artifacts via exfiltration from the victim system. Access may be obtained through Valid Accounts, exposed storage, misconfigured permissions, or exploitation of systems that store or serve the AI artifacts. Alternatively, adversaries may collect Outputs from AI Models to Replicate AI Model Behavior without obtaining the original model artifacts. Model training approaches include knowledge distillation, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning. Adversaries may use a stolen or replicated model internally themselves, sell or distribute it, offer competing AI services, or incorporate its capabilities into their own products without compensating the model owner. Possession of the model also allows adversaries to modify or fine-tune it and operate outside the original provider's access controls, usage restrictions, and monitoring. These activities may reduce the victim's competitive advantage and undermine the exclusivity of proprietary AI capabilities.

## Mitigations (3)

- [AML.M0005: Control Access to AI Models and Data at Rest](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0005.md): Establish access controls on internal model registries and limit internal access to production models.
- [AML.M0012: Encrypt Sensitive Information](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0012.md): Encrypt sensitive data such as AI models to protect against adversaries attempting to access sensitive data.
- [AML.M0017: AI Model Distribution Methods](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0017.md): Deploying AI models to edge devices can increase the attack surface of the system.
