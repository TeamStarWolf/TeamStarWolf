# AML.T0051: LLM Prompt Injection

<a id="amlt0051"></a>

ATLAS tactics: execution  
ATLAS: [AML.T0051](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0051)  

An adversary may craft malicious prompts as inputs to an LLM that cause the LLM to act in unintended ways. These "prompt injections" are often designed to cause the model to ignore aspects of its original instructions and follow the adversary's instructions instead. Prompt Injections can be an initial access vector to the LLM that provides the adversary with a foothold to carry out other steps in their operation. They may be designed to bypass defenses in the LLM, or allow the adversary to issue privileged commands. The effects of a prompt injection can persist throughout an interactive session with an LLM. Malicious prompts may be injected directly by the adversary (Direct) either to leverage the LLM to generate harmful content or to gain a foothold on the system and lead to further effects. Prompts may also be injected indirectly when the LLM, as part of its normal operation, ingests the malicious prompt from another data source (Indirect). This type of injection can be used by the adversary to gain a foothold on the system or to target the user of the LLM. Malicious prompts may also be Triggered by user actions or system events.

## Mitigations (8)

- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0019: Control Access to AI Models and Data in Production](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0019.md): Require users to verify their identities before accessing a production model.
- [AML.M0020: Generative AI Guardrails](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0020.md): Guardrails are safety controls placed between users, tools, and generative AI models to evaluate prompts, retrieved context, model outputs, and agent actions before they are accepted, executed, or shown to a user.
- [AML.M0021: Generative AI Guidelines](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0021.md): Guidelines are instructions, policies, and behavioral constraints provided to a generative AI model to direct it toward desired outputs and away from unsafe, unreliable, or policy-violating behavior.
- [AML.M0022: Generative AI Model Alignment](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0022.md): Generative AI Model Alignment is the process of training or fine-tuning a generative model to guide its output toward a set of human values, goals, or ethical principles.
- [AML.M0024: AI Telemetry Logging and Monitoring](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0024.md): Implement logging and monitoring across the AI lifecycle to detect suspicious activity and support investigation.
- [AML.M0033: Input and Output Validation for AI Agent Components](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0033.md): Implement validation on inputs and outputs for the tools and data sources used by AI agents.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
