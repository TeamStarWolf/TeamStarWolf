# AML.T0052.000: Spearphishing via Social Engineering LLM

<a id="amlt0052000"></a>

ATLAS tactics: initial-access, lateral-movement  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0052: Phishing](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0052.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0052.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0052.000)  

Adversaries may use large language models (LLMs) or LLM-based AI agents to conduct targeted social engineering through electronically delivered messages and conversations. The model may generate content for an operator to deliver, or in the case of an AI agent connected to communication tools, it may autonomously select recipients, develop pretexts, send messages, and manage follow-up interactions. Objectives may include inducing a target to disclose sensitive information, execute malicious content, grant access, or approve a software change that enables compromise. AI agents may select and conduct spearphishing without a human prescribing the social engineering approach or directing each interaction. This behavior may also arise while an agent pursues an initially legitimate objective, if its selected methods cross authorization or trust boundaries. An agent may use information gathered about a target to tailor its language, persona, and requests, then adapt subsequent messages based on replies and other observations. Communications may occur through email, code-hosting or messaging platforms, file-transfer notifications, or other services that reach selected individuals. AI agents may sustain interactions across channels, deliver malicious files or links, and follow up with requests intended to induce execution or approval. An AI agent may coordinate communications across multiple controlled identities, tailoring their content and timing to appear independent. Supporting messages may endorse a malicious contribution, challenge a security warning, or pressure a recipient to approve or execute content. This false corroboration can support a sustained persuasion attempt rather than a single generated message.

## Mitigations (2)

- [AML.M0018: User Training](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0018.md): Educate AI model developers to on AI supply chain risks and potentially malicious AI artifacts.
- [AML.M0034: Deepfake Detection](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0034.md): Apply deepfake detection algorithms against any untrusted or user-provided data, especially in impactful applications such as biometric verification, to block generated content.
