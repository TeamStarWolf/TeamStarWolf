# AML.T0059: Erode Dataset Integrity

<a id="amlt0059"></a>

ATLAS tactics: impact  
ATLAS: [AML.T0059](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0059)  

Adversaries may poison or manipulate portions of a dataset to reduce its usefulness, reduce trust, and cause users to waste resources correcting errors.

## Mitigations (2)

- [AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0007.md): Detect and remove or remediate poisoned training data.
- [AML.M0025: Maintain AI Dataset Provenance](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0025.md): Maintain a detailed history of datasets used for AI applications.
