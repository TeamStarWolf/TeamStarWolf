# AML.T0061: LLM Prompt Self-Replication

<a id="amlt0061"></a>

ATLAS tactics: persistence  
ATLAS: [AML.T0061](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0061)  

Adversaries may introduce a prompt designed to replicate itself in a large language model's (LLM) output. The reproduced instructions may appear in generated messages, documents, source-code, files, or other artifacts. The prompt may direct the AI system to copy the instructions themselves or reproduce enclosing content that contains them. The self-replication instructions may be paired with additional malicious objectives, such as collecting sensitive information, modifying files, or invoking tools on the adversary's behalf. Self-replicating prompts can persist in artifacts on the victim system. When retrieved and ingested by another LLM, the adversary's instructions are re-introduced, potentially long after the original interaction has ended. These retained copies may allow the adversary's influence to survive removal of the original malicious prompt payload without requiring the adversary to deliver it again. Adversaries may deliver self-replicating prompts via LLM Prompt Injection. Replicated instructions may support LLM Prompt Propagation when the generated content is passed to and ingested by other AI systems or components. The payload may also include instructions to bypass model safeguards through LLM Jailbreak or pursue objectives such as Exfiltration.

## Mitigations (3)

- [AML.M0020: Generative AI Guardrails](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0020.md): Guardrails are safety controls placed between users, tools, and generative AI models to evaluate prompts, retrieved context, model outputs, and agent actions before they are accepted, executed, or shown to a user.
- [AML.M0021: Generative AI Guidelines](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0021.md): Guidelines are instructions, policies, and behavioral constraints provided to a generative AI model to direct it toward desired outputs and away from unsafe, unreliable, or policy-violating behavior.
- [AML.M0022: Generative AI Model Alignment](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0022.md): Generative AI Model Alignment is the process of training or fine-tuning a generative model to guide its output toward a set of human values, goals, or ethical principles.
