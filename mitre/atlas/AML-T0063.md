# AML.T0063: Discover AI Model Outputs

<a id="amlt0063"></a>

ATLAS tactics: discovery  
ATLAS: [AML.T0063](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0063)  

Adversaries may discover what information an AI model or service exposes through its outputs. They may investigate both the intended responses and additional model-generated information exposed through the surrounding application. Relevant outputs may include predictions, scores, rankings, representations, generated content, reasoning, explanations, or evaluation results. Adversaries may inspect responses and related records and submit targeted inputs to determine what information is available, how it relates to the inputs, and what it reveals about the model's behavior. These observations may help identify useful signals for subsequent collection and exploitation. Identified outputs may guide collection of Outputs from AI Models or Training Data Information from AI Models, support Recover AI Model Internals, or be used to Replicate AI Model Behavior.

## Mitigations (4)

- [AML.M0002: Predictive AI Output Obfuscation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0002.md): Reduce the fidelity and amount of information returned by predictive AI inference endpoints to make model discovery, extraction, replication, and black-box adversarial-example optimization more difficult.
- [AML.M0012: Encrypt Sensitive Information](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0012.md): Encrypt sensitive data such as AI models to protect against adversaries attempting to access sensitive data.
- [AML.M0017: AI Model Distribution Methods](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0017.md): Deploying AI models to edge devices can increase the attack surface of the system.
- [AML.M0019: Control Access to AI Models and Data in Production](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0019.md): Require users to verify their identities before accessing a production model.
