# AML.T0065: LLM Prompt Crafting

<a id="amlt0065"></a>

ATLAS tactics: ai-attack-adaptation  
ATLAS: [AML.T0065](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0065)  

Adversaries may use their acquired knowledge of the target generative AI system to craft prompts that bypass its defenses and allow malicious instructions to be executed. They may iterate on prompts to ensure that they work as intended consistently. Adversaries may also craft systematic sets of prompts to collect Outputs from AI Models for selected model capabilities and subsequently Replicate AI Model Behavior. These sets may use repeated structures and variations to obtain domain-specific responses, reasoning explanations (including hidden chain-of-thought), rubric-based evaluations, or other tasks suitable for training other models. Reported distillation campaigns have targeted reasoning, coding, tool use, computer use, and other capabilities. Individual prompts need not contain an obviously malicious instruction or bypass a guardrail. Their adversarial purpose may only be discernable in aggregate.
