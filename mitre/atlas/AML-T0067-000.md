# AML.T0067.000: Citations

<a id="amlt0067000"></a>

ATLAS tactics: defense-evasion  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0067: LLM Trusted Output Components Manipulation](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0067.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0067.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0067.000)  

Adversaries may manipulate the citations provided in an AI system's response, in order to make it appear trustworthy. Variants include providing the wrong citation, making up a new citation, or providing the right citation but for adversary-provided data.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0020: Generative AI Guardrails](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0020.md): Guardrails are safety controls placed between users, tools, and generative AI models to evaluate prompts, retrieved context, model outputs, and agent actions before they are accepted, executed, or shown to a user.
