# AML.T0072: Cyber Communication Channel

<a id="amlt0072"></a>

ATLAS tactics: command-and-control  
ATLAS: [AML.T0072](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0072)  

Adversaries may establish or use cyber communication channels for command and control. A channel may use any network protocol, service, repository, relay, or other intermediary and may operate synchronously or asynchronously. See the ATT&CK Command and Control tactic for techniques describing specific protocols, services, relays, tunneling methods, and communication patterns.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0043: AI Agent External Resource Lifecycle Management](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0043.md): Organizations should control external resources created or acquired by AI agents during execution by treating those resources as attributable, lifecycle-managed assets.
