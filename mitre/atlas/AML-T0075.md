# AML.T0075: Enterprise Resource Discovery

<a id="amlt0075"></a>

ATLAS tactics: discovery  
ATLAS: [AML.T0075](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0075)  

Adversaries enumerate enterprise resources, including accounts, systems, files, applications, services, and cloud assets, to identify targets or access paths.
