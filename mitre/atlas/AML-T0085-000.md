# AML.T0085.000 — RAG Databases

<a id="amlt0085000"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** collection
**Sub-technique of:** [AML.T0085 — Data from AI Services](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0085.md)
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0085.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0085.000)

Adversaries may prompt the AI service to retrieve data from a RAG database. This can include the majority of an organization's internal documents.
