# AML.T0085.003: System Instructions

<a id="amlt0085003"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0085: Data from AI Services](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0085.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0085.003](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0085.003)  

Adversaries may collect non-public instructions used to configure the behavior of an AI model or agent. These may include system prompts or application-specific rules governing tasks, tool use, response formatting, and handling of sensitive information. Adversaries may request that the service reproduce, summarize, or transform its instructions, or use LLM Prompt Injection or LLM Jailbreak to induce disclosure. Collection may reveal complete instructions or selected portions across multiple responses. Collected instructions may expose proprietary application logic, internal workflows, tool descriptions, or sensitive information embedded in the instructions. This information may support replication of application behavior or help adversaries tailor subsequent attacks.

## Mitigations (5)

- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0020: Generative AI Guardrails](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0020.md): Guardrails are safety controls placed between users, tools, and generative AI models to evaluate prompts, retrieved context, model outputs, and agent actions before they are accepted, executed, or shown to a user.
- [AML.M0021: Generative AI Guidelines](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0021.md): Guidelines are instructions, policies, and behavioral constraints provided to a generative AI model to direct it toward desired outputs and away from unsafe, unreliable, or policy-violating behavior.
- [AML.M0022: Generative AI Model Alignment](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0022.md): Generative AI Model Alignment is the process of training or fine-tuning a generative model to guide its output toward a set of human values, goals, or ethical principles.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
