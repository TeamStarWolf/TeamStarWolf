# AML.T0085: Data from AI Services

<a id="amlt0085"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
ATLAS: [AML.T0085](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0085)  

Adversaries may use their access to victim AI-enabled services to collect proprietary or otherwise sensitive information available to the system. As organizations adopt generative and agentic AI in centralized services for accessing an organization's data they become increasingly valuable targets for adversaries. Adversaries may prompt a large language model or AI agent to retrieve internal documents from RAG Databases or obtain records from organizational systems through AI Agent Tools. They may also elicit protected information from users' Conversation Context and Memory or recover hidden System Instructions. The information available to the AI system may exceed what the adversary can access directly, including data belonging to other users or resources accessible through the service's permissions. Adversaries may request information directly, supply contextual cues, or construct multi-turn interactions that encourage disclosure. Ordinary-looking requests may succeed where authorization, context isolation, or disclosure controls are inadequate, while LLM Jailbreak may allow the adversary to overcome restrictions or model guardrails. Collected information may be returned through an Exfiltration via AI Inference API or Exfiltration via AI User Interface, or transmitted elsewhere through Exfiltration via AI Agent Tool Invocation.

## Mitigations (6)

- [AML.M0022: Generative AI Model Alignment](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0022.md): Generative AI Model Alignment is the process of training or fine-tuning a generative model to guide its output toward a set of human values, goals, or ethical principles.
- [AML.M0024: AI Telemetry Logging and Monitoring](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0024.md): Implement logging and monitoring across the AI lifecycle to detect suspicious activity and support investigation.
- [AML.M0026: Manage AI Agent Service Access](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0026.md): Limit service AI agents--agents using independently assigned service or workload permissions--to explicitly authorized workflows.
- [AML.M0027: Manage User-Delegated AI Agent Access](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0027.md): Limit user-delegated AI agents to explicitly authorized tasks and permissions.
- [AML.M0028: Enforce Access Controls for AI Agent Tools](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0028.md): Limit AI agent tool access to each caller's authorized, task-required operations and resources, including when tools are shared across agents, users, or tenants.
- [AML.M0032: Segmentation of AI Agent Components](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0032.md): Define enforceable security boundaries around AI agent tools, data sources, identities, and execution environments.
