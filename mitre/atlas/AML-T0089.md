# AML.T0089 — Enterprise Environment Discovery

<a id="amlt0089"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** discovery
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0089](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0089)

Adversaries inspect enterprise configurations, activity, permissions, and security controls to understand the environment and adapt subsequent actions.
