# AML.T0092 — Manipulate User LLM Chat History

<a id="amlt0092"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** defense-evasion  
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0092](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0092)  

Adversaries may manipulate a user's large language model (LLM) chat history to cover the tracks of their malicious behavior. They may hide persistent changes they have made to the LLM's behavior, or obscure their attempts at discovering private information about the user. To do so, adversaries may delete or edit existing messages or create new threads as part of their coverup. This is feasible if the adversary has the victim's authentication tokens for the backend LLM service or if they have direct access to the victim's chat interface.
