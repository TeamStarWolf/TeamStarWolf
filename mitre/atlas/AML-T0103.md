# AML.T0103 — Deploy AI Agent

<a id="amlt0103"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** execution  
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0103](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0103)  

Adversaries may launch AI agents in the victim's environment to execute actions on their behalf. AI agents may have access to a wide range of tools and data sources, as well as permissions to access and interact with other services and systems in the victim's environment. The adversary may leverage these capabilities to carry out their operations. Adversaries may configure the AI agent by providing an initial system prompt and granting access to tools, effectively defining their goals for the agent to achieve.
