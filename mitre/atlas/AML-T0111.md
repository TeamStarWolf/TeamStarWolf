# AML.T0111 — AI Supply Chain Reputation Inflation

<a id="amlt0111"></a>

**ATLAS tactics:** defense-evasion  
**ATLAS:** [AML.T0111](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0111)  

AI Supply Chain Reputation Inflation is the process of building or leveraging genuinely credible-looking trust signals to increase the perceived legitimacy of AI supply chain components, with the goal of driving adoption of malicious or compromised assets. Adversaries use established developer accounts with a history of legitimate projects and contributions to publish AI models, datasets, packages, and MCP servers that appear trustworthy.
