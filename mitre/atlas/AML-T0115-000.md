# AML.T0115.000: Datasets

<a id="amlt0115000"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0115: Publish Poisoned AI Artifacts](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0115.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0115.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0115.000)  

Adversaries may publish poisoned datasets intended for training or fine-tuning AI models. The dataset may be newly created or a modified variant of a legitimate dataset and may contain manipulated samples, labels, annotations, or metadata. Adversaries may distribute poisoned datasets through dataset repositories, code repositories, file-sharing services, or compromised data sources. A victim that incorporates the dataset into a training pipeline may be affected by Training Data Poisoning, potentially through Supply Chain Compromise.

## Mitigations (2)

- [AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0007.md): Detect, quarantine, and remove or remediate poisoned data before it is used to train an AI model.
- [AML.M0042: Threat Intelligence Program](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0042.md): A Threat Intelligence Program enables organizations to proactively collect, analyze, and share information on threats to AI and cyber systems by leveraging internal observations and external reporting.
