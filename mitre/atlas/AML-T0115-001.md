# AML.T0115.001: Models

<a id="amlt0115001"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0115: Publish Poisoned AI Artifacts](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0115.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0115.001](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0115.001)  

Adversaries may publish poisoned AI models through model registries, code repositories, or other model distribution channels. The model may be newly created or a modified variant of a legitimate model and may contain manipulated weights, configurations, architecture, serialized code, or other components that produce malicious behavior or execute malicious code. Victims may subsequently download and integrate the model through Supply Chain Compromise.

## Mitigations (3)

- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0016: Vulnerability Scanning](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0016.md): Vulnerability scanning is used to find potentially exploitable software vulnerabilities to remediate them.
- [AML.M0042: Threat Intelligence Program](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0042.md): A Threat Intelligence Program enables organizations to proactively collect, analyze, and share information on threats to AI and cyber systems by leveraging internal observations and external reporting.
