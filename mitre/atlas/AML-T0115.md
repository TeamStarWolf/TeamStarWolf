# AML.T0115: Publish Poisoned AI Artifacts

<a id="amlt0115"></a>

ATLAS tactics: resource-development  
ATLAS: [AML.T0115](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0115)  

Adversaries may create or modify AI artifacts and publish them through public or shared distribution channels to facilitate compromise of downstream AI systems. Poisoned AI artifacts may include datasets, models, and AI agent tools containing malicious content, behaviors, code, or configurations. Adversaries may publish novel artifacts or malicious variants of legitimate artifacts through dataset or model repositories, package registries, source code repositories, tool hubs, or remotely hosted services. Victims may subsequently acquire and integrate these artifacts through Supply Chain Compromise.

## Mitigations (3)

- [AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0007.md): Detect, quarantine, and remove or remediate poisoned data before it is used to train an AI model.
- [AML.M0016: Vulnerability Scanning](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0016.md): Vulnerability scanning is used to find potentially exploitable software vulnerabilities to remediate them.
- [AML.M0042: Threat Intelligence Program](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0042.md): A Threat Intelligence Program enables organizations to proactively collect, analyze, and share information on threats to AI and cyber systems by leveraging internal observations and external reporting.
