# AML.T0135: Outputs from AI Models

<a id="amlt0135"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
ATLAS: [AML.T0135](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0135)  

Adversaries may gather, retain, and organize outputs from an AI model or service as material for subsequent adversarial operations. Systematic selection and aggregation of responses can produce datasets or observations useful for analyzing the target, reproducing its behavior, or developing further attacks. Collected material may include predictions and scores, embeddings or other exposed feature representations, generated content, reasoning traces, grades or preference judgments, and model-generated plans or tool calls. Outputs may be acquired directly from inference responses or indirectly from product-generated artifacts and records in which they are embedded or retained. Discover AI Model Outputs may help adversaries identify available outputs and useful signals before collection. Adversaries may perform LLM Prompt Crafting to elicit the outputs they require for follow-on actions. The resulting material may support collection of Training Data Information from AI Models, Recover AI Model Internals, or Replicate Behavior from Model Outputs. Adversaries may also use Centralized AI Service Request Routing, Indicator Removal, and AI Service Request Metadata Sanitization to obscure coordinated collection activity.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
