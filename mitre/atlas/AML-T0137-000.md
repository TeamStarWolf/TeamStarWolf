# AML.T0137.000: Infer Membership

<a id="amlt0137000"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0137: Training Data Information from AI Models](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0137.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0137.000](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0137.000)  

Adversaries may infer whether a particular data sample was included in an AI model's training dataset. Samples may include records, text passages, images, or audio samples. Establishing membership may reveal sensitive associations, participation in a private dataset, or use of proprietary information. Membership inference is a data privacy concern because identifying a sample's inclusion may reveal sensitive associations or participation in a private dataset, even without recovering the underlying data. Methods may analyze prediction scores, token probabilities, loss values, generated responses, or other differences in model behavior. Adversaries may use statistical tests, shadow models, or auxiliary datasets to distinguish likely training members from nonmembers.

## Mitigations (7)

- [AML.M0002: Predictive AI Output Obfuscation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0002.md): Reduce the fidelity and amount of information returned by predictive AI inference endpoints to make model discovery, extraction, replication, and black-box adversarial-example optimization more difficult.
- [AML.M0004: Limit AI Service Query Volume and Rate](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0004.md): Limit the number and rate of requests that users can submit to an AI service.
- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0024: AI Telemetry Logging and Monitoring](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0024.md): Implement logging and monitoring across the AI lifecycle to detect suspicious activity and support investigation.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
- [AML.M0041: Privacy-Preserving AI Training and Inference](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0041.md): Use privacy-preserving mechanisms throughout model training and inference to limit disclosure of sensitive training or inference data.
