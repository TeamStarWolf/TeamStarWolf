# AML.T0137.001: Infer Properties

<a id="amlt0137001"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0137: Training Data Information from AI Models](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0137.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0137.001](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0137.001)  

Adversaries may infer sensitive global properties of the data used to train an AI model. Properties may include dataset provenance, population composition, relative class proportions, domain coverage, collection period, or the presence of a category or group. The adversary may compare output distributions, fit analytical or learned estimators, use auxiliary datasets, or contrast models trained with different known properties. The target is a property of the training data rather than membership of an individual record. Inferred properties of the training dataset may help adversaries identify a model's likely origin, distinguish between candidate models, identify underrepresented populations or domains, and tailor subsequent attacks to suspected weaknesses.

## Mitigations (2)

- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
- [AML.M0041: Privacy-Preserving AI Training and Inference](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0041.md): Use privacy-preserving mechanisms throughout model training and inference to limit disclosure of sensitive training or inference data.
