# AML.T0137.003: Extract Memorized Data

<a id="amlt0137003"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
Sub-technique of: [AML.T0137: Training Data Information from AI Models](/mitre/atlas/AML-T0137.md)  
ATLAS: [AML.T0137.003](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0137.003)  

Adversaries may elicit and recover sensitive content memorized by a generative AI model during training or fine-tuning. Recovered material may include complete training examples or recognizable portions of text, source code, or images, potentially exposing personal information, private communications, or proprietary material. Recovery may take the form of verbatim reproduction or near-duplicate outputs that reveal specific training content. Adversaries may supply partial passages, contextual cues, or repeated and varied inputs that encourage reproduction of memorized content. They may generate and filter candidate outputs or compare them with available reference material to identify likely training examples. Researchers have recovered verbatim training passages through language-model queries, including from production systems, and extracted memorized training images from diffusion models.

## Mitigations (4)

- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0020: Generative AI Guardrails](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0020.md): Guardrails are safety controls placed between users, tools, and generative AI models to evaluate prompts, retrieved context, model outputs, and agent actions before they are accepted, executed, or shown to a user.
- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
- [AML.M0041: Privacy-Preserving AI Training and Inference](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0041.md): Use privacy-preserving mechanisms throughout model training and inference to limit disclosure of sensitive training or inference data.
