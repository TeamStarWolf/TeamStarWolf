# AML.T0137: Training Data Information from AI Models

<a id="amlt0137"></a>

ATLAS tactics: collection  
ATLAS: [AML.T0137](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0137)  

Adversaries may obtain sensitive information about the data used to train or fine-tune an AI model by analyzing its outputs or behavior. This can expose private associations, personally identifiable information, or proprietary data, creating data privacy concerns and supporting subsequent adversarial operations. Adversaries may determine whether particular samples were used in training through Infer Membership, identify aggregate characteristics of the training dataset through Infer Properties, infer sensitive attributes or reconstruct representative examples through Invert AI Model, or recover retained training content through Extract Memorized Data.

## Mitigations (2)

- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
- [AML.M0041: Privacy-Preserving AI Training and Inference](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0041.md): Use privacy-preserving mechanisms throughout model training and inference to limit disclosure of sensitive training or inference data.
