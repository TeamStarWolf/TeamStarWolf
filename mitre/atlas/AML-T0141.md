# AML.T0141: AI Service Request Metadata Sanitization

<a id="amlt0141"></a>

ATLAS tactics: defense-evasion  
ATLAS: [AML.T0141](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0141)  

Adversaries may remove or replace identifying information in request metadata before submitting requests to an AI service to impede attribution and correlation of coordinated activity. Sanitization may be enforced automatically in clients, relays, aggregators, or centralized routing infrastructure. Metadata may include client or device identifiers, session identifiers, account identifiers, client software identifiers, forwarding headers, and attribution markers embedded in request bodies. Metadata fields may be sanitized via removal, or replacement with normalized, randomized, or generated values. The NSA, CISA, and FBI reported removal of organizational identifiers in industrial-scale distillation campaigns, with indicators including the disappearance of previously consistent metadata and generic or randomized patterns replacing organizational markers.
