# AML.T0144: Recover AI Model Internals

<a id="amlt0144"></a>

ATLAS tactics: discovery, collection  
ATLAS: [AML.T0144](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0144)  

Adversaries may infer or recover non-public AI model internals through analysis of model behavior or exposed information. Targets may include architecture, layer dimensions, hyperparameters, decision-tree structure, projection matrices, or numerical parameters. Methods may use strategically selected queries, equation solving, numerical reconstruction, or statistical identification. Recovery may be partial, approximate, or exact.

## Mitigations (1)

- [AML.M0040: Adaptive AI Response Degradation](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0040.md): Reduce the utility of AI model outputs for unauthorized model replication, knowledge distillation, or inference about training data by dynamically modifying responses when suspected extraction activity or privacy attacks are detected.
