# AML.T0145: Poison AI Agent Learning Environment

<a id="amlt0145"></a>

ATLAS tactics: persistence  
ATLAS: [AML.T0145](https://atlas.mitre.org/techniques/AML.T0145)  

Adversaries may manipulate an environment in which an AI agent interacts so that observations, feedback, rewards, actions, or resulting interactions are incorporated into model training to cause persistent changes to the behavior of an AI system. This allows adversary's influence to persist beyond the original interaction and affect subsequent tasks. An adversary may alter webpages, tool or API responses, elements in simulated environments, task descriptions, reward signals, evaluator outputs, or other environmental content used during AI agent learning or adaptation. The manipulated interactions may be incorporated into model fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, synthetic training corpora, agent memories, other persistent components. Poison AI Agent Learning Environment targets the process that generates or selects learning experience, without requiring direct adversarial access to training datasets, model parameters, or stored agent artifacts. The resulting experience may appear legitimate because it was produced by an authorized agent operating through an expected interface. Once introduced, the adversary's influence may remain effective even after the manipulated environmental content is removed or is no longer encountered. An adversary may use this technique to introduce backdoors, increase compliance with malicious instructions, distort policies or preferences, teach unsafe tool use, manipulate reward-seeking behavior. The malicious behavior may be activated later by an environmental trigger or during ordinary agent operation.

## Mitigations (6)

- [AML.M0007: Sanitize Training Data](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0007.md): Detect, quarantine, and remove or remediate poisoned data before it is used to train an AI model.
- [AML.M0008: Validate AI Model](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0008.md): Evaluate AI models against defined functional requirements before deployment and throughout their operational lifecycle.
- [AML.M0020: Generative AI Guardrails](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0020.md): Guardrails are safety controls placed between users, tools, and generative AI models to evaluate prompts, retrieved context, model outputs, and agent actions before they are accepted, executed, or shown to a user.
- [AML.M0022: Generative AI Model Alignment](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0022.md): Generative AI Model Alignment is the process of training or fine-tuning a generative model to guide its output toward a set of human values, goals, or ethical principles.
- [AML.M0025: Maintain AI Dataset Provenance](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0025.md): Maintain a detailed history of datasets used for AI applications.
- [AML.M0035: AI Red Team](/mitre/atlas/AML-M0035.md): Establish an AI red team responsible for conducting recurring, authorized, and threat-informed red-teaming exercises to identify and remediate vulnerabilities in AI-enabled systems before deployment and throughout operation.
