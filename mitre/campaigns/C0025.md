# C0025 — 2016 Ukraine Electric Power Attack

<a id="c0025"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0025](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0025)  
**Active:** 2016-12-01 – 2016-12-01  

[2016 Ukraine Electric Power Attack](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0025) was a [Sandworm Team](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0034) campaign during which they used [Industroyer](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0604) malware to target and disrupt distribution substations within the Ukrainian power grid. This campaign was the second major public attack conducted against Ukraine by [Sandworm Team](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0034).
