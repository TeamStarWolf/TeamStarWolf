# C0034 — 2022 Ukraine Electric Power Attack

<a id="c0034"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0034](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0034)  
**Active:** 2022-06-01 – 2022-10-01  

The [2022 Ukraine Electric Power Attack](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0034) was a [Sandworm Team](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0034) campaign that used a combination of GOGETTER, Neo-REGEORG, [CaddyWiper](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0693), and living of the land (LotL) techniques to gain access to a Ukrainian electric utility to send unauthorized commands from their SCADA system.
