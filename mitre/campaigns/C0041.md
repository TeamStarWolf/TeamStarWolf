# C0041 — FrostyGoop Incident

<a id="c0041"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0041](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0041)  
**Active:** 2024-01-01 – 2024-01-01  

[FrostyGoop Incident](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0041) took place in January 2024 against a municipal district heating company in Ukraine. Following initial access via likely exploitation of external facing services, [FrostyGoop](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1165) was used to manipulate ENCO control systems via legitimate Modbus commands to impact the delivery of heating services to Ukrainian civilians.
