# C0042: Outer Space

<a id="c0042"></a>

ATT&CK: [C0042](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0042)  
Active: 2021-01-01 to 2021-12-01  

[Outer Space](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0042) was a campaign conducted by [OilRig](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0049) throughout 2021 that used the [SampleCheck5000](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1168) downloader and [Solar](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1166) backdoor to target Israeli organizations.
