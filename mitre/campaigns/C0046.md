# C0046: ArcaneDoor

<a id="c0046"></a>

ATT&CK: [C0046](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0046)  
Active: 2023-07-01 to 2024-04-01  

[ArcaneDoor](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0046) is a campaign targeting networking devices from Cisco and other vendors between July 2023 and April 2024, primarily focused on government and critical infrastructure networks. [ArcaneDoor](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0046) is associated with the deployment of the custom backdoors [Line Runner](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1188) and [Line Dancer](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1186). [ArcaneDoor](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0046) is attributed to a group referred to as UAT4356 or STORM-1849, and is assessed to be a state-sponsored campaign.
