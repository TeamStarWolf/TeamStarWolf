# C0047 — RedDelta Modified PlugX Infection Chain Operations

<a id="c0047"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0047](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0047)  
**Active:** 2023-07-01 – 2024-12-01  

[RedDelta Modified PlugX Infection Chain Operations](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0047) was executed by [Mustang Panda](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0129) from mid-2023 through the end of 2024 against multiple entities in East and Southeast Asia. [RedDelta Modified PlugX Infection Chain Operations](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0047) involved phishing to deliver malicious files or links to users prompting follow-on installer downloads to load [PlugX](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0013) on victim machines in a persistent state.

## Attributed to

[Mustang Panda](/mitre/groups/G0129.md)
