# C0056 — RedPenguin

<a id="c0056"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0056](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0056)  
**Active:** 2024-07-01 – 2025-03-01  

The [RedPenguin](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0056) project was launched by Juniper in July 2024 to investigate reported malware infections of Juniper MX Series routers. [RedPenguin](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0056) activity was separately attributed to [UNC3886](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1048) and included the deployment of multiple custom versions of the publicly-available TINYSHELL backdoor on Juniper routers.
