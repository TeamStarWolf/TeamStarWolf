# C0060 — Operation AkaiRyū

<a id="c0060"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0060](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0060)  
**Active:** 2004-06-01 – 2004-09-01  

[Operation AkaiRyū](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0060) (Japanese for RedDragon) was a cyberespionage spearphishing campaign conducted by [MirrorFace](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1054) between June and September 2024 against entities in Japan and Central Europe. [Operation AkaiRyū](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0060) notably included the first reported targeting of a European entity by [MirrorFace](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1054), as well as their use of [UPPERCUT](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0275), which was thought to be exclusive to [menuPass](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0045).
