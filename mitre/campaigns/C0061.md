# C0061 — Operation Digital Eye

<a id="c0061"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [C0061](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0061)  
**Active:** 2024-06-01 – 2024-07-01  

[Operation Digital Eye](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0061) was conducted in June and July of 2024 by suspected People's Republic of China (PRC)-nexus threat actors targeting business-to-business IT service providers in Southern Europe. [Operation Digital Eye](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0061) activity included the use of Visual Studio Code tunnels for command and control (C2) and custom lateral movement capabilities. Overlaps in tooling between Digital Eye and previous China-nexus campaigns, Operation Soft Cell and Operation Tainted Love, indicate the potential use of shared vendors or digital quartermasters.
