# C0064: AI-Assisted Breach of Mexico's Government Infrastructure

<a id="c0064"></a>

ATT&CK: [C0064](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0064)  
Active: 2025-12-01 to 2026-02-01  

The [AI-Assisted Breach of Mexico's Government Infrastructure](https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0064) is a campaign where human operators used Claude Code AI and OpenAI GPT GPT-4.1 AI to perform reconnaissance, vulnerability exploitation, privilege escalation, discovery, credential harvesting, and exfiltration operations. The attack impacted several Mexican government institutions including the Federal Tax Authority (Servicio de Administracion Tributaria), Mexico State Government (Estado de Mexico), Mexico City Civil Registry (Registro Civil de CDMX), Jalisco State Government, National Electoral Institute (Instituto Nacional Electoral), Michoacan State Government, SADM Monterrey Municipal Water Utility (Agua y Drenaje), Tamaulipas State Government, and the Mexico City Health Department (Salud CDMX).
