# CAPEC-397 — Cloning Magnetic Strip Cards

<a id="capec-397"></a>

**Abstraction:** Detailed  
**Status:** Draft  

An attacker duplicates the data on a Magnetic strip card (i.e. 'swipe card' or 'magstripe') to gain unauthorized access to a physical location or a person's private information. Magstripe cards encode data on a band of iron-based magnetic particles arrayed in a stripe along a rectangular card.
