# CAPEC-416 — Manipulate Human Behavior

<a id="capec-416"></a>

**Abstraction:** Meta  
**Typical severity:** Medium  
**Likelihood:** Medium

An adversary exploits inherent human psychological predisposition to influence a targeted individual or group to solicit information or manipulate the target into performing an action that serves the adversary's interests. **Mitigations:** ::An organization should provide regular, robust cybersecurity training to its employees to prevent successful social engineering attacks.::
