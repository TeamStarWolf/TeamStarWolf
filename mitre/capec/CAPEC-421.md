# CAPEC-421 — Influence Perception of Authority

<a id="capec-421"></a>

**Abstraction:** Detailed  
**Typical severity:** Low  
**Likelihood:** High

An adversary uses a social engineering technique to convey a sense of authority that motivates the target to reveal specific information or take specific action. There are various techniques for producing a sense of authority during ordinary modes of communication. One common method is impersonation.
