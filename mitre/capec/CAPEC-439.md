# CAPEC-439 — Manipulation During Distribution

<a id="capec-439"></a>

**Abstraction:** Meta  
**Status:** Draft  

An attacker undermines the integrity of a product, software, or technology at some stage of the distribution channel. The core threat of modification or manipulation during distribution arise from the many stages of distribution, as a product may traverse multiple suppliers and integrators as the final asset is delivered.
