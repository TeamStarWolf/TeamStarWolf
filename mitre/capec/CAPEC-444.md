# CAPEC-444 — Development Alteration

<a id="capec-444"></a>

**Abstraction:** Standard  
**Typical severity:** High  
**Likelihood:** Medium

An adversary modifies a technology, product, or component during its development to acheive a negative impact once the system is deployed. The goal of the adversary is to modify the system in such a way that the negative impact can be leveraged when the system is later deployed.
