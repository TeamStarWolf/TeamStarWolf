# CAPEC-569 — Collect Data as Provided by Users

<a id="capec-569"></a>

**Abstraction:** Standard  
**Status:** Draft  

An attacker leverages a tool, device, or program to obtain specific information as provided by a user of the target system. This information is often needed by the attacker to launch a follow-on attack. This attack is different than Social Engineering as the adversary is not tricking or deceiving the user.
