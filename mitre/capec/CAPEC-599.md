# CAPEC-599 — Terrestrial Jamming

<a id="capec-599"></a>

**Abstraction:** Detailed  
**Typical severity:** High  
**Likelihood:** Low  
**Status:** Draft  

In this attack pattern, the adversary transmits disruptive signals in the direction of the target's consumer-level satellite dish (as opposed to the satellite itself). The transmission disruption occurs in a more targeted range. Portable terrestrial jammers have a range of 3-5 kilometers in urban areas and 20 kilometers in rural areas.
