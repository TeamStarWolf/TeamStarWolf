# D3FEND: Email Filtering

<a id="email-filtering"></a>

D3FEND tactic: Isolate  
Digital artifacts: Email  

Filtering incoming email traffic based on specific criteria.

## ATT&CK techniques countered (5)

- [T0865: Spearphishing Attachment](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T0865): filters
- [T1114.001: Local Email Collection](/mitre/techniques/T1114-001.md): filters. Adversaries may target user email on local systems to collect sensitive information.
- [T1534: Internal Spearphishing](/mitre/techniques/T1534.md): filters. After they already have access to accounts or systems within the environment, adversaries may use internal spearphishing to gain access to additional information or compromise other users within the same organization.
- [T1566.001: Spearphishing Attachment](/mitre/techniques/T1566-001.md): filters. Adversaries may send spearphishing emails with a malicious attachment in an attempt to gain access to victim systems.
- [T1566.002: Spearphishing Link](/mitre/techniques/T1566-002.md): filters. Adversaries may send spearphishing emails with a malicious link in an attempt to gain access to victim systems.
