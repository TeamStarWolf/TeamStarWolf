# S9021 — DOWNIISSA

**Classification:** community / emerging  
**Type:** malware  
**Local ref:** `S9021` (not official ATT&CK)  
**Platforms:** Windows  

DOWNIISSA is a shellcode downloader that has been used by [MirrorFace](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1054) since at least 2022 to deploy payloads, including the LODEINFO backdoor.
