**Classification:** community / emerging  
**Type:** malware  
**Local ref:** `S9030` (not official ATT&CK)

SameCoin is a multi-platform wiper with Windows and Android versions that has been used by [WIRTE](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0090) to target entities in the Middle East including in Israel.
