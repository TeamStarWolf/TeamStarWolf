# F1041 — PIN-code Peeking

<a id="f3-f1041"></a>

**F3 tactics:** Initial Access, Reconnaissance  
**Fight Fraud Framework:** [F1041](https://ctid.mitre.org/fraud/techniques/F1041)  

Fraud actors observe or obtain personal identification numbers (PINs). Use of hidden cameras, skimming devices, or other covert methods to capture PINs at ATMs, point-of-sale terminals, or other PIN-entry locations. Obtained PIN numbers used with other stolen information to compromise the victim's accounts or conduct fraudulent transactions.
