# G0087 — APT39

<a id="g0087"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [G0087](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0087)  
**Aliases:** ITG07, Chafer, Remix Kitten  

[APT39](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0087) is one of several names for cyber espionage activity conducted by the Iranian Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS) through the front company Rana Intelligence Computing since at least 2014. [APT39](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0087) has primarily targeted the travel, hospitality, academic, and telecommunications industries in Iran and across Asia, Africa, Europe, and North America to track individuals and entities considered to be a threat by the MOIS.
