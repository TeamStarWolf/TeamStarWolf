# G0107 — Whitefly

<a id="g0107"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [G0107](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0107)  

[Whitefly](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0107) is a cyber espionage group that has been operating since at least 2017. The group has targeted organizations based mostly in Singapore across a wide variety of sectors, and is primarily interested in stealing large amounts of sensitive information. The group has been linked to an attack against Singapore’s largest public health organization, SingHealth.
