# G0142 — Confucius

<a id="g0142"></a>

**ATT&CK:** [G0142](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0142)  
**Aliases:** Confucius APT  

[Confucius](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0142) is a cyber espionage group that has primarily targeted military personnel, high-profile personalities, business persons, and government organizations in South Asia since at least 2013. Security researchers have noted similarities between [Confucius](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0142) and [Patchwork](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0040), particularly in their respective custom malware code and targets.
