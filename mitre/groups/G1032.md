# G1032: INC Ransom

<a id="g1032"></a>

ATT&CK: [G1032](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1032)  
Aliases: GOLD IONIC  

[INC Ransom](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1032) is a ransomware and data extortion threat group associated with the deployment of [INC Ransomware](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1139) that has been active since at least July 2023. [INC Ransom](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1032) has targeted organizations worldwide most commonly in the industrial, healthcare, and education sectors in the US and Europe.
