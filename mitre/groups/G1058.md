# G1058: The Gentlemen

<a id="g1058"></a>

ATT&CK: [G1058](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1058)  
Aliases: Storm-2697, GOLD SHERWOOD  

[The Gentlemen](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1058) is a group that maintains the [The Gentlemen Ransomware](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9051)-as-a-service (RaaS). [The Gentlemen](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1058) was first observed in mid-2025 who had posed [The Gentlemen Ransomware](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9051) as a closed ransomware and shortly later opened the ransomware as a RaaS to affiliates in September 2025.
