# G1059: NIMBUS MANTICORE

<a id="g1059"></a>

ATT&CK: [G1059](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1059)  
Aliases: UNC1549, TA455, COBALT SMOKEY  

[NIMBUS MANTICORE](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1059) is a State Sponsored cyber espionage group associated with the Iranian Regime specifically the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). [NIMBUS MANTICORE](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1059) has employed social engineering attacks to include HR related themes and has leveraged custom malware against its victims.
