# S0041 — Wiper

<a id="s0041"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S0041](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0041)  

[Wiper](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0041) is a family of destructive malware used in March 2013 during breaches of South Korean banks and media companies.
