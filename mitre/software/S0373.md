# S0373: Astaroth

<a id="s0373"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S0373](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0373)  
Platforms: Windows  
Aliases: Guildma  

[Astaroth](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0373) is a Trojan and information stealer known to affect companies in Europe, Brazil, and throughout Latin America. It has been known publicly since at least late 2017.
