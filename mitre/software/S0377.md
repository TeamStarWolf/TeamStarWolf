# S0377 — Ebury

<a id="s0377"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S0377](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0377)  
**Platforms:** Linux  

[Ebury](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0377) is an OpenSSH backdoor and credential stealer targeting Linux servers and container hosts developed by [Windigo](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0124). [Ebury](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0377) is primarily installed through modifying shared libraries (`.so` files) executed by the legitimate OpenSSH program. First seen in 2009, [Ebury](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0377) has been used to maintain a botnet of servers, deploy additional malware, and steal cryptocurrency wallets, credentials, and credit card details.
