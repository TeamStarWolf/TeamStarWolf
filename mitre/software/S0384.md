# S0384: Dridex

<a id="s0384"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S0384](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0384)  
Platforms: Windows  
Aliases: Bugat v5  

[Dridex](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0384) is a prolific banking Trojan that first appeared in 2014. By December 2019, the US Treasury estimated [Dridex](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0384) had infected computers in hundreds of banks and financial institutions in over 40 countries, leading to more than $100 million in theft. [Dridex](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0384) was created from the source code of the Bugat banking Trojan (also known as Cridex).
