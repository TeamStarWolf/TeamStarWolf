# S0449 — Maze

<a id="s0449"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S0449](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0449)  
**Platforms:** Windows  

[Maze](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0449) ransomware, previously known as "ChaCha", was discovered in May 2019. In addition to encrypting files on victim machines for impact, [Maze](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0449) operators conduct information stealing campaigns prior to encryption and post the information online to extort affected companies.
