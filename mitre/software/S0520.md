# S0520: BLINDINGCAN

<a id="s0520"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S0520](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0520)  
Platforms: Windows  

[BLINDINGCAN](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0520) is a remote access Trojan that has been used by the North Korean government since at least early 2020 in cyber operations against defense, engineering, and government organizations in Western Europe and the US.
