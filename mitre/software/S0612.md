# S0612: WastedLocker

<a id="s0612"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S0612](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0612)  
Platforms: Windows  

[WastedLocker](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0612) is a ransomware family attributed to [Indrik Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0119) that has been used since at least May 2020. [WastedLocker](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0612) has been used against a broad variety of sectors, including manufacturing, information technology, and media.
