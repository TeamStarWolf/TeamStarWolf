# S1023 — CreepyDrive

<a id="s1023"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S1023](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1023)  
**Platforms:** Windows, Office Suite  

[CreepyDrive](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1023) is a custom implant has been used by [POLONIUM](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1005) since at least early 2022 for C2 with and exfiltration to actor-controlled OneDrive accounts. [POLONIUM](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1005) has used a similar implant called CreepyBox that relies on actor-controlled DropBox accounts.
