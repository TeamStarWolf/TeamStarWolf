# S1058: Prestige

<a id="s1058"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S1058](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1058)  
Platforms: Windows  

[Prestige](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1058) ransomware has been used by [Sandworm Team](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0034) since at least March 2022, including against transportation and related logistics industries in Ukraine and Poland in October 2022.
