# S1111 — DarkGate

<a id="s1111"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S1111](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1111)  
**Platforms:** Windows  

[DarkGate](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1111) first emerged in 2018 and has evolved into an initial access and data gathering tool associated with various criminal cyber operations. Written in Delphi and named "DarkGate" by its author, [DarkGate](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1111) is associated with credential theft, cryptomining, cryptotheft, and pre-ransomware actions. DarkGate use increased significantly starting in 2022 and is under active development by its author, who provides it as a Malware-as-a-Service offering.
