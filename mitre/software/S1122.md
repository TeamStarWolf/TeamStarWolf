# S1122: Mispadu

<a id="s1122"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S1122](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1122)  
Platforms: Windows  

[Mispadu](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1122) is a banking trojan written in Delphi that was first observed in 2019 and uses a Malware-as-a-Service (MaaS) business model. This malware is operated, managed, and sold by the [Malteiro](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1026) cybercriminal group. [Mispadu](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1122) has mainly been used to target victims in Brazil and Mexico, and has also had confirmed operations throughout Latin America and Europe.
