# S1151 — ZeroCleare

<a id="s1151"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S1151](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1151)  
**Platforms:** Windows  
**Aliases:** ZEROCLEAR  

[ZeroCleare](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1151) is a wiper malware that has been used in conjunction with the [RawDisk](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0364) driver since at least 2019 by suspected Iran-nexus threat actors including activity targeting the energy and industrial sectors in the Middle East and political targets in Albania.
