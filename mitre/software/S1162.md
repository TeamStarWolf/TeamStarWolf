# S1162: Playcrypt

<a id="s1162"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S1162](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1162)  
Platforms: Windows  
Aliases: Play  

[Playcrypt](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1162) is a ransomware that has been used by [Play](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1040) since at least 2022 in attacks against against the business, government, critical infrastructure, healthcare, and media sectors in North America, South America, and Europe. [Playcrypt](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1162) derives its name from adding the.play extension to encrypted files and has overlap with tactics and tools associated with Hive and Nokoyawa ransomware and infrastructure associated with Quantum ransomware.
