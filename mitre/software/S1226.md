# S1226: BOOKWORM

<a id="s1226"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S1226](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1226)  
Platforms: Windows  

[BOOKWORM](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1226) is a modular trojan known to be leveraged by [Mustang Panda](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0129) and was first observed utilized in 2015. [BOOKWORM](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S1226) was later updated in late 2021 and the fall of 2022 to launch shellcode represented as UUID parameters.
