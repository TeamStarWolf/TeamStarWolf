# S9023 — HiddenFace

<a id="s9023"></a>

**Type:** malware  
**ATT&CK:** [S9023](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9023)  
**Platforms:** Windows  
**Aliases:** NOOPDOOR  

[HiddenFace](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9023) is a modular backdoor developed and used exclusively by [MirrorFace](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1054) since at least 2021. [HiddenFace](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9023) can communicate both actively and passively and has been used against political and academic targets.
