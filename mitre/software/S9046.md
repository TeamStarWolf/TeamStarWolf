# S9046: IOControl

<a id="s9046"></a>

Type: malware  
ATT&CK: [S9046](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9046)  

IOControl is a malware that targeted Linux ARM-based IoT/OT devices including Hikvision IP cameras, D-Link firewalls and routers, Unitronics PLCs and HMIs, Orpak and Gasboy fuel management systems, and Baicells LTE controllers across the United States and Israel. The malware has been attributed to [CyberAv3ngers](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1027) which is suspected to be affiliated with the Iranian Government Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC).(Citation: Claroty-IOCONTROL)(Citation: Dragos 2025 Year in Review)(Citation: Armis-IOCONTROL)
