# T1003.008 — /etc/passwd and /etc/shadow

<a id="t1003008"></a>

**Tactics:** credential-access  
**Platforms:** Linux  
**ATT&CK:** [T1003.008](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1003/008)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1003 — OS Credential Dumping](/mitre/techniques/T1003.md)  

Adversaries may attempt to dump the contents of <code>/etc/passwd</code> and <code>/etc/shadow</code> to enable offline password cracking.

## Mitigations (2)

- [M1026 — Privileged Account Management](/mitre/mitigations/M1026.md) — Privileged Account Management focuses on implementing policies, controls, and tools to securely manage privileged accounts (e.g., SYSTEM, root, or administrative accounts).
- [M1027 — Password Policies](/mitre/mitigations/M1027.md) — Set and enforce secure password policies for accounts to reduce the likelihood of unauthorized access.
