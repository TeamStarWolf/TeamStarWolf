# T1069 — Permission Groups Discovery

<a id="t1069"></a>

**Tactics:** discovery  
**Platforms:** Containers, IaaS, Identity Provider, Linux, macOS, Office Suite, SaaS, Windows  
**ATT&CK:** [T1069](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1069)  

Adversaries may attempt to discover group and permission settings. This information can help adversaries determine which user accounts and groups are available, the membership of users in particular groups, and which users and groups have elevated permissions.

## Detection

**Detection strategies:**

- Behavioral Detection of Permission Groups Discovery

**Analytics (3):**

- **Analytic 0507** (Windows) — `WinEventLog:Security EventCode=4688` → Process Creation; `WinEventLog:PowerShell EventCode=4103, 4104, 4105, 4106` → Command Execution

## Adversary usage

**Threat groups (6):** [APT3](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0022), [TA505](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0092), [APT41](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0096), [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015), [FIN13](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1016), [Volt Typhoon](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1017)

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [APT3](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0022) (aka Gothic Panda, Pirpi, UPS Team, Buckeye) — APT3 is a China-based threat group that researchers have attributed to China's Ministry of State Security.
- [TA505](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0092) (aka Hive0065, Spandex Tempest, CHIMBORAZO) — TA505 is a cyber criminal group that has been active since at least 2014.
- [APT41](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0096) (aka Wicked Panda, Brass Typhoon, BARIUM) — APT41 is a threat group that researchers have assessed as Chinese state-sponsored espionage group that also conducts financially-motivated operations.
- [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015) (aka Roasted 0ktapus, Octo Tempest, Storm-0875, UNC3944) — Scattered Spider is a native English-speaking cybercriminal group active since at least 2022.
- [FIN13](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1016) (aka Elephant Beetle) — FIN13 is a financially motivated cyber threat group that has targeted the financial, retail, and hospitality industries in Mexico and Latin America, as early as 2016.
- [Volt Typhoon](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1017) (aka BRONZE SILHOUETTE, Vanguard Panda, DEV-0391, UNC3236) — Volt Typhoon is a People's Republic of China (PRC) state-sponsored actor that has been active since at least 2021 primarily targeting critical infrastructure organizations in the US and its territories including Guam.

**Software/tools (6):** [MURKYTOP](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0233), [TrickBot](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0266), [Carbon](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0335), [ShimRatReporter](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0445), [IcedID](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0483), [Siloscape](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S0623)
