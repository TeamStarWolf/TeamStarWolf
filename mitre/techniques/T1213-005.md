# T1213.005 — Messaging Applications

<a id="t1213005"></a>

**Tactics:** collection  
**Platforms:** SaaS, Office Suite  
**ATT&CK:** [T1213.005](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1213/005)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1213 — Data from Information Repositories](/mitre/techniques/T1213.md)  

Adversaries may leverage chat and messaging applications, such as Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, and Slack, to mine valuable information.

## Mitigations (3)

- [M1017 — User Training](/mitre/mitigations/M1017.md) — User Training involves educating employees and contractors on recognizing, reporting, and preventing cyber threats that rely on human interaction, such as phishing, social engineering, and other manipulative techniques.
- [M1047 — Audit](/mitre/mitigations/M1047.md) — Auditing is the process of recording activity and systematically reviewing and analyzing the activity and system configurations.
- [M1060 — Out-of-Band Communications Channel](/mitre/mitigations/M1060.md) — Establish secure out-of-band communication channels to ensure the continuity of critical communications during security incidents, data integrity attacks, or in-network communication failures.

## Detection

**Detection strategies:**

- Detecting Unauthorized Collection from Messaging Applications in SaaS and Office Environments

**Analytics (2):**

- **Analytic 1565** (SaaS) — `saas:slack conversations.history, files.list, users.info, audit_logs` → Application Log Content; `m365:signinlogs UserLoggedIn` → Logon Session Creation
- **Analytic 1566** (Office Suite) — `m365:unified TeamsMessagesAccessedViaEDiscovery, TeamsGraphMessageExport` → Application Log Content; `m365:signinlogs UserLoggedIn` → Logon Session Creation

## Data sources & telemetry (2)

**Application Log Content**, **Logon Session Creation**

## Adversary usage

**Threat groups (3):** [Fox Kitten](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0117), [LAPSUS$](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1004), [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015)

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [Fox Kitten](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0117) (aka UNC757, Parisite, Pioneer Kitten, RUBIDIUM) — Fox Kitten is threat actor with a suspected nexus to the Iranian government that has been active since at least 2017 against entities in the Middle East, North Africa, Europe, Australia, and North America.
- [LAPSUS$](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1004) (aka DEV-0537, Strawberry Tempest) — LAPSUS$ is cyber criminal threat group that has been active since at least mid-2021.
- [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015) (aka Roasted 0ktapus, Octo Tempest, Storm-0875, UNC3944) — Scattered Spider is a native English-speaking cybercriminal group active since at least 2022.
