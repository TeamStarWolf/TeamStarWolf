# T1489 — Service Stop

<a id="t1489"></a>

**Tactics:** impact  
**Platforms:** ESXi, IaaS, Linux, macOS, Windows  
**ATT&CK:** [T1489](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1489)  

Adversaries may stop or disable services on a system to render those services unavailable to legitimate users. Stopping critical services or processes can inhibit or stop response to an incident or aid in the adversary's overall objectives to cause damage to the environment.

## Detection

**Detection strategies:**

- Behavioral Detection for Service Stop across Platforms

**Analytics (4):**

- **Analytic 0061** (Windows) — `WinEventLog:Sysmon EventCode=1` → Process Creation; `WinEventLog:Security EventCode=4672` → Logon Session Metadata; `WinEventLog:System EventCode=7036` → Service Creation; `WinEventLog:Sysmon EventCode=4` → Service Metadata
- **Analytic 0062** (Linux) — `auditd:SYSCALL execve of systemctl or service stop` → Process Creation; `auditd:SYSCALL unlink/unlinkat on service binaries or data targets` → File Deletion; `linux:syslog service stopped messages` → Service Metadata
- **Analytic 0063** (macOS) — `macos:unifiedlog launchctl disable or bootout calls` → Service Metadata; `auditd:SYSCALL execve of launchctl or pkill` → Process Creation
- **Analytic 0064** (ESXi) — `esxi:hostd Stop VM or disable service events via vim-cmd` → Service Metadata; `esxi:hostd Log entries indicating VM powered off or forcibly terminated` → Process Termination
