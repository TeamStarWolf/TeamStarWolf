# T1583.004 — Server

<a id="t1583004"></a>

**Tactics:** resource-development  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1583.004](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1583/004)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1583 — Acquire Infrastructure](/mitre/techniques/T1583.md)  

Adversaries may buy, lease, rent, or obtain physical servers that can be used during targeting. Use of servers allows an adversary to stage, launch, and execute an operation.

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [Sandworm Team](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0034) (aka ELECTRUM, Telebots, IRON VIKING, BlackEnergy (Group)) — Sandworm Team is a destructive threat group that has been attributed to Russia's General Staff Main Intelligence Directorate (GRU) Main Center for Special Technologies (GTsST) military unit 74455.
- [GALLIUM](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0093) (aka Granite Typhoon) — GALLIUM is a cyberespionage group that has been active since at least 2012, primarily targeting telecommunications companies, financial institutions, and government entities in Afghanistan, Australia, Belgium, Cambodia…
- [Kimsuky](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0094) (aka Black Banshee, Velvet Chollima, Emerald Sleet, THALLIUM) — Kimsuky is a North Korea-based cyber espionage group that has been active since at least 2012.
- [Earth Lusca](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1006) (aka TAG-22, Charcoal Typhoon, CHROMIUM, ControlX) — Earth Lusca is a suspected China-based cyber espionage group that has been active since at least April 2019.
- [CURIUM](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1012) (aka Crimson Sandstorm, TA456, Tortoise Shell, Yellow Liderc) — CURIUM is an Iranian threat group, first reported in September 2019 and active since at least July 2018, targeting IT service providers in the Middle East.
- [Mustard Tempest](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1020) (aka DEV-0206, TA569, GOLD PRELUDE, UNC1543) — Mustard Tempest is an initial access broker that has operated the SocGholish distribution network since at least 2017.
