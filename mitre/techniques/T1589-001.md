# T1589.001 — Credentials

<a id="t1589001"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1589.001](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1589/001)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1589 — Gather Victim Identity Information](/mitre/techniques/T1589.md)  

Adversaries may gather credentials that can be used during targeting. Account credentials gathered by adversaries may be those directly associated with the target victim organization or attempt to take advantage of the tendency for users to use the same passwords across personal and business accounts.

## Adversary usage

**Threat groups (5):** [APT28](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0007), [Magic Hound](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0059), [Leviathan](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0065), [Chimera](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0114), [LAPSUS$](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1004)

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [APT28](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0007) (aka IRON TWILIGHT, SNAKEMACKEREL, Swallowtail, Group 74) — APT28 is a threat group that has been attributed to Russia's General Staff Main Intelligence Directorate (GRU) 85th Main Special Service Center (GTsSS) military unit 26165.
- [Magic Hound](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0059) (aka TA453, COBALT ILLUSION, Charming Kitten, ITG18) — Magic Hound is an Iranian-sponsored threat group that conducts long term, resource-intensive cyber espionage operations, likely on behalf of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.
- [Leviathan](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0065) (aka MUDCARP, Kryptonite Panda, Gadolinium, BRONZE MOHAWK) — Leviathan is a Chinese state-sponsored cyber espionage group that has been attributed to the Ministry of State Security's (MSS) Hainan State Security Department and an affiliated front company.
- [Chimera](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0114) — Chimera is a suspected China-based threat group that has been active since at least 2018 targeting the semiconductor industry in Taiwan as well as data from the airline industry.
- [LAPSUS$](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1004) (aka DEV-0537, Strawberry Tempest) — LAPSUS$ is cyber criminal threat group that has been active since at least mid-2021.
