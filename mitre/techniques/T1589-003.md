# T1589.003 — Employee Names

<a id="t1589003"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1589.003](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1589/003)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1589 — Gather Victim Identity Information](/mitre/techniques/T1589.md)  

Adversaries may gather employee names that can be used during targeting. Employee names be used to derive email addresses as well as to help guide other reconnaissance efforts and/or craft more-believable lures.

- [Kimsuky](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0094) (aka Black Banshee, Velvet Chollima, Emerald Sleet, THALLIUM) — Kimsuky is a North Korea-based cyber espionage group that has been active since at least 2012.
- [Silent Librarian](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0122) (aka TA407, COBALT DICKENS) — Silent Librarian is a group that has targeted research and proprietary data at universities, government agencies, and private sector companies worldwide since at least 2013.
