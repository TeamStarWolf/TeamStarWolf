# T1593.001 — Social Media

<a id="t1593001"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1593.001](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1593/001)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1593 — Search Open Websites/Domains](/mitre/techniques/T1593.md)  

Adversaries may search social media for information about victims that can be used during targeting. Social media sites may contain various information about a victim organization, such as business announcements as well as information about the roles, locations, and interests of staff.

- [Contagious Interview](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1052) (aka DeceptiveDevelopment, Gwisin Gang, Tenacious Pungsan, DEV#POPPER) — Contagious Interview is a North Korea–aligned threat group active since 2023.
