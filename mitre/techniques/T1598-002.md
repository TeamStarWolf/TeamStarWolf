# T1598.002 — Spearphishing Attachment

<a id="t1598002"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1598.002](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1598/002)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1598 — Phishing for Information](/mitre/techniques/T1598.md)  

Adversaries may send spearphishing messages with a malicious attachment to elicit sensitive information that can be used during targeting. Spearphishing for information is an attempt to trick targets into divulging information, frequently credentials or other actionable information.

## Mitigations (2)

- [M1017 — User Training](/mitre/mitigations/M1017.md) — User Training involves educating employees and contractors on recognizing, reporting, and preventing cyber threats that rely on human interaction, such as phishing, social engineering, and other manipulative techniques.
- [M1054 — Software Configuration](/mitre/mitigations/M1054.md) — Software configuration refers to making security-focused adjustments to the settings of applications, middleware, databases, or other software to mitigate potential threats.
