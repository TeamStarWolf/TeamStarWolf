# T1598.003 — Spearphishing Link

<a id="t1598003"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1598.003](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1598/003)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1598 — Phishing for Information](/mitre/techniques/T1598.md)  

Adversaries may send spearphishing messages with a malicious link to elicit sensitive information that can be used during targeting. Spearphishing for information is an attempt to trick targets into divulging information, frequently credentials or other actionable information.

## Mitigations (2)

- [M1017 — User Training](/mitre/mitigations/M1017.md) — User Training involves educating employees and contractors on recognizing, reporting, and preventing cyber threats that rely on human interaction, such as phishing, social engineering, and other manipulative techniques.
- [M1054 — Software Configuration](/mitre/mitigations/M1054.md) — Software configuration refers to making security-focused adjustments to the settings of applications, middleware, databases, or other software to mitigate potential threats.

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [APT28](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0007) (aka IRON TWILIGHT, SNAKEMACKEREL, Swallowtail, Group 74) — APT28 is a threat group that has been attributed to Russia's General Staff Main Intelligence Directorate (GRU) 85th Main Special Service Center (GTsSS) military unit 26165.
- [Sandworm Team](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0034) (aka ELECTRUM, Telebots, IRON VIKING, BlackEnergy (Group)) — Sandworm Team is a destructive threat group that has been attributed to Russia's General Staff Main Intelligence Directorate (GRU) Main Center for Special Technologies (GTsST) military unit 74455.
- [Dragonfly](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0035) (aka TEMP.Isotope, DYMALLOY, Berserk Bear, TG-4192) — Dragonfly is a cyber espionage group that has been attributed to Russia's Federal Security Service (FSB) Center 16.
- [Patchwork](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0040) (aka Hangover Group, Dropping Elephant, Chinastrats, MONSOON) — Patchwork is a cyber espionage group that was first observed in December 2015.
- [APT32](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0050) (aka SeaLotus, OceanLotus, APT-C-00, Canvas Cyclone) — APT32 is a suspected Vietnam-based threat group that has been active since at least 2014.
- [Magic Hound](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0059) (aka TA453, COBALT ILLUSION, Charming Kitten, ITG18) — Magic Hound is an Iranian-sponsored threat group that conducts long term, resource-intensive cyber espionage operations, likely on behalf of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps.
