# T1598.004 — Spearphishing Voice

<a id="t1598004"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1598.004](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1598/004)  
**Sub-technique of:** [T1598 — Phishing for Information](/mitre/techniques/T1598.md)  

Adversaries may use voice communications to elicit sensitive information that can be used during targeting. Spearphishing for information is an attempt to trick targets into divulging information, frequently credentials or other actionable information.

## Mitigations (1)

- [M1017 — User Training](/mitre/mitigations/M1017.md) — User Training involves educating employees and contractors on recognizing, reporting, and preventing cyber threats that rely on human interaction, such as phishing, social engineering, and other manipulative techniques.
