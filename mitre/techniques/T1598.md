# T1598 — Phishing for Information

<a id="t1598"></a>

**Tactics:** reconnaissance  
**Platforms:** PRE  
**ATT&CK:** [T1598](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1598)  

Adversaries may send phishing messages to elicit sensitive information that can be used during targeting. Phishing for information is an attempt to trick targets into divulging information, frequently credentials or other actionable information.

## Mitigations (2)

- [M1017 — User Training](/mitre/mitigations/M1017.md) — User Training involves educating employees and contractors on recognizing, reporting, and preventing cyber threats that rely on human interaction, such as phishing, social engineering, and other manipulative techniques.

**Threat groups (5):** [APT28](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0007), [Kimsuky](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0094), [ZIRCONIUM](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0128), [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015), [Moonstone Sleet](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1036)

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [APT28](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0007) (aka IRON TWILIGHT, SNAKEMACKEREL, Swallowtail, Group 74) — APT28 is a threat group that has been attributed to Russia's General Staff Main Intelligence Directorate (GRU) 85th Main Special Service Center (GTsSS) military unit 26165. This group has been active since at least 2004.
- [Kimsuky](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0094) (aka Black Banshee, Velvet Chollima, Emerald Sleet, THALLIUM) — Kimsuky is a North Korea-based cyber espionage group that has been active since at least 2012. The group initially targeted South Korean government agencies, think tanks, and subject-matter experts in various fields.
- [ZIRCONIUM](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0128) (aka APT31, Violet Typhoon) — ZIRCONIUM is a threat group operating out of China, active since at least 2017, that has targeted individuals associated with the 2020 US presidential election and prominent leaders in the international affairs…
