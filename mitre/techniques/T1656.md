# T1656 — Impersonation

<a id="t1656"></a>

**Tactics:** defense-evasion  
**Platforms:** Linux, macOS, Office Suite, SaaS, Windows  
**ATT&CK:** [T1656](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1656)  

Adversaries may impersonate a trusted person or organization in order to persuade and trick a target into performing some action on their behalf.

## Mitigations (2)

- [M1017 — User Training](/mitre/mitigations/M1017.md) — User Training involves educating employees and contractors on recognizing, reporting, and preventing cyber threats that rely on human interaction, such as phishing, social engineering, and other manipulative techniques.
- [M1019 — Threat Intelligence Program](/mitre/mitigations/M1019.md) — A Threat Intelligence Program enables organizations to proactively identify, analyze, and act on cyber threats by leveraging internal and external data sources.

## Detection

**Analytics (5):**

- **Analytic 0792** (Windows) — `WinEventLog:Security EventCode=4624, 4648` → Logon Session Creation; `m365:unified SendOnBehalf/SendAs: Emails sent where the sending identity mismatches account ownership` → Application Log Content
- **Analytic 0793** (Linux) — `auditd:SYSCALL execve: Processes executing sendmail/postfix with forged headers` → Command Execution; `Application:Mail Mismatch between authenticated username and From header in email` → Application Log Content
- **Analytic 0794** (macOS) — `macos:unifiedlog Mail.app or third-party clients sending messages with mismatched From headers` → Application Log Content
- **Analytic 0795** (SaaS) — `gcp:workspaceaudit SendAs: Outbound messages with alias identities that differ from primary account` → Application Log Content
- **Analytic 0796** (Office Suite) — `m365:unified SendOnBehalf/SendAs: Office Suite initiated messages using impersonated identities` → Application Log Content

## Adversary usage

**Threat groups (8):** [Kimsuky](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0094), [APT41](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0096), [LAPSUS$](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1004), [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015), [Saint Bear](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1031), [APT42](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1044), [Storm-1811](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1046), [Contagious Interview](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1052)

_Notable groups seen using this technique:_

- [Kimsuky](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0094) (aka Black Banshee, Velvet Chollima, Emerald Sleet, THALLIUM) — Kimsuky is a North Korea-based cyber espionage group that has been active since at least 2012.
- [APT41](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0096) (aka Wicked Panda, Brass Typhoon, BARIUM) — APT41 is a threat group that researchers have assessed as Chinese state-sponsored espionage group that also conducts financially-motivated operations.
- [LAPSUS$](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1004) (aka DEV-0537, Strawberry Tempest) — LAPSUS$ is cyber criminal threat group that has been active since at least mid-2021.
- [Scattered Spider](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1015) (aka Roasted 0ktapus, Octo Tempest, Storm-0875, UNC3944) — Scattered Spider is a native English-speaking cybercriminal group active since at least 2022.
- [Saint Bear](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1031) (aka Storm-0587, TA471, UAC-0056, Lorec53) — Saint Bear is a Russian-nexus threat actor active since early 2021, primarily targeting entities in Ukraine and Georgia.
- [APT42](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1044) — APT42 is an Iranian-sponsored threat group that conducts cyber espionage and surveillance.
