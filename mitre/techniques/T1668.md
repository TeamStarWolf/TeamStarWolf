# T1668 — Exclusive Control

<a id="t1668"></a>

**Tactics:** persistence  
**Platforms:** Linux, macOS, Windows  
**ATT&CK:** [T1668](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1668)  

Adversaries who successfully compromise a system may attempt to maintain persistence by “closing the door” behind them – in other words, by preventing other threat actors from initially accessing or maintaining a foothold on the same system.

## Detection

**Detection strategies:**

- Detection Strategy for Exclusive Control

**Analytics (3):**

- **Analytic 0045** (Windows) — `WinEventLog:Security EventCode=4688` → Process Creation; `WinEventLog:Sysmon EventCode=5` → Process Termination
- **Analytic 0046** (Linux) — `auditd:SYSCALL execve: Commands like systemctl stop <service>, service <service> stop, or kill -9 <pid>` → Command Execution; `linux:syslog Unexpected termination of daemons or critical services not aligned with admin change tickets` → Process Termination
- **Analytic 0047** (macOS) — `macos:unifiedlog launchctl unload, kill, or pkill commands affecting daemons or background services` → Command Execution; `macos:osquery process_termination: Unexpected termination of processes tied to vulnerable or high-value services` → Process Termination
