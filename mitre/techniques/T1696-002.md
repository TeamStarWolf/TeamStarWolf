# T1696.002: Avoid CAPTCHA

> New in ATT&CK v20.0. MITRE added this technique in the current release. The overview, defender notes, references and related ATLAS techniques on this page are the library's own, last reviewed 2026-11-03; the NIST control and corpus signal data do not cover it yet.

Many bot defenses only show a challenge when a session already looks suspicious. Avoiding the CAPTCHA means never reaching that threshold: replaying real browser fingerprints, pacing requests, moving the pointer and typing with human timing, and rotating through residential addresses.

SentinelLabs' 2025 AkiraBot report describes a spam framework that targeted hCAPTCHA and reCAPTCHA and carried browser fingerprints so its traffic would look like a legitimate visitor's. SentinelLabs assessed that it targeted more than 400,000 websites.

ATT&CK's definition: Adversaries may avoid CAPTCHA challenges by mimicking human behavior. Automation tests, such as CAPTCHA, are typically used by defenders to detect and prevent bot and spam traffic from accessing protected webpages.

## Detection

Analytics (1):

- Analytic 2105 (SaaS): No target-side behavioral Detection Strategy exists for Avoid CAPTCHA based solely on CAPTCHA telemetry.

Defender notes (library): MITRE publishes an analytic that notes there is no target-side behavioral detection based only on CAPTCHA telemetry, so detection has to come from the rest of the session. Look for sessions that are never challenged yet show automation traits (headless or instrumented browser markers, fingerprints that repeat across many addresses, identical navigation paths), contact-form or chat-widget submissions that arrive in volume with generated text, and traffic from residential proxy ranges that does not match the site's normal audience.
